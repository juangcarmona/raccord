## Rule

Every user sees the emails they sent themselves; seeing other users' sent emails, the complete communication log, or incoming notes requires the corresponding rights.

## Rationale

Communication is personal by default.

## Examples

- A planner sees their own invitation emails but not the account manager's quotation emails.

## Exceptions

None.
