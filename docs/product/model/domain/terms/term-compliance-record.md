## Definition

The obligation, created together with each issued invoice, to report that invoice to the tax authority, with its reporting status.

## Distinguish From

Billing record: the regulated record content sent to the tax authority, produced from the compliance record when reporting exists.

## Usage

Cannot be deleted; its status changes as reporting progresses.
