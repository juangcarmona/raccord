## Definition

An amount asked from the customer to insure rented equipment, defined as a percentage of the gross rental equipment price of the project (before discounts) and shown on quotes and invoices.

## Distinguish From

Refundable deposit, which is a fixed returnable amount.

## Usage

Set per project in the financial overview; booked on the insurance ledger.
