## Rule

The price of planned lines marked optional is not added to the quotation total; options are listed among other equipment by default, or in a separate section when the quotation layout says so.

## Rationale

Options are offers, not commitments.

## Examples

A quotation with a 1,000 EUR base and a 200 EUR optional projector totals 1,000 EUR.

## Exceptions

None.
