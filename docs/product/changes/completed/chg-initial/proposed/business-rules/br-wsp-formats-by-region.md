## Rule

The 12- or 24-hour time format follows the country chosen at set-up, and the date order follows the user's computer regional settings.

## Rationale

Regional familiarity.

## Examples

- A user whose computer uses month-first dates sees 07/31/2026 while a colleague sees 31/07/2026.

## Exceptions

None.
