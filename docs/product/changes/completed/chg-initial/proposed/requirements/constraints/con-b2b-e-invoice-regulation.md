## Constraint

Invoices between businesses and professionals in Spain must be issued and received as structured electronic invoices (EN 16931 semantic model; UBL for the public solution) and the recipient must report acceptance or rejection and full payment with its date, normally within four natural days excluding weekends and national holidays (Ley 18/2022 art. 12, RD 238/2026, Order HAC/1028/2026).

## Rationale

Imposed by Spanish law; nominal start dates 2027-10-06 for companies over 8 million euros turnover and 2028-10-06 for the rest, subject to later changes.

## Consequences

Raccord must send, receive and track structured invoices and their statuses.
