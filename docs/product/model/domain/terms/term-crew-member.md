## Definition

A person the company can plan on work: an employee or a freelancer. Attributes: first, middle and last name, email (also the login identity), phone, address, description, user role (rights and permissions), login permission, record folder, skill tags, whether the person is shown and plannable in crew planning, whether the record may be planned several times at once (agency record), administrative data (birth date, passport number, social security number, bank account, hours in contract, contract date, driving licence, emergency contact, and company name, chamber of commerce number and VAT identification number for people working through their own company), the profile-ownership setting, linked crew rates and a default rate, crew documents, open invitations and a personal schedule.

## Distinguish From

Contact person: a party record used commercially, not plannable. Vehicle: a plannable resource that is not a person. Agency crew record: a crew member record standing for a supplier of people.

## Usage

Planned on functions and shifts, invited, given rates and documents; registers hours.
