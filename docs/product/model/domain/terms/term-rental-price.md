## Definition

The amount, excluding VAT, charged to the customer per day of rental of one unit of a rental item. It is the starting price copied onto planned lines; discount group and factor group determine how it is adjusted for longer periods and customers.

## Distinguish From

- List price: what the company originally paid, used for valuation.

## Usage

Entered at creation (also in imports and bulk updates); defaulted onto quotes and invoices through planned lines.
