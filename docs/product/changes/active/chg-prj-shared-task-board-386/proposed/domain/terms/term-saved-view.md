## Definition

A named, reusable combination of overview settings: date range, tags, project status selection, filters and columns (other overviews offer their own options). It has a visibility (personal or shared with others). Earlier presets and column sets survive as views and can be merged into one view.

## Distinguish From

Filter: a single condition. Task board grouping: may be personal or saved to the board.

## Usage

Quickly switching datasets in overviews and boards.
