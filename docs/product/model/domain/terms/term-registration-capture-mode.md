## Definition

How a time registration is created: manual (enter from and until, link activities later), from planned activities (choose scheduled functions or shifts of the last 24 hours; times are filled in from them) or clock in/out (a running timer that continues in the background until stopped).

## Distinguish From

Hour type: classifies the time, not how it was entered.

## Usage

Registration on the web and the mobile web app.
