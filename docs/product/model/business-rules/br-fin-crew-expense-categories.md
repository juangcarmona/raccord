## Rule

Crew expenses exist only in the four predefined categories catering, travel, accommodation and other; companies cannot add categories, and some locales show meals and lodging as names for catering and accommodation.

## Rationale

Comparable expense reporting across projects.

## Examples

A parking fee is recorded as travel.

## Exceptions

None.
