## Rule

Signing up for a posted job does not check the crew member's availability or conflicting planning.

## Rationale

Self-service speed; the planner remains responsible.

## Examples

See SB-CRW-JOB-SIGNUP-DESPITE-CONFLICT.

## Exceptions

None.
