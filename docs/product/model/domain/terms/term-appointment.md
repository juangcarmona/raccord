## Definition

A named personal commitment of one or more people in their schedules: name, period, optional colour, location, recurrence and remark. An appointment can make the person unavailable for other work.

## Distinguish From

Shift: plannable internal work with a required quantity. Availability entry: no named activity.

## Usage

Meetings, personal commitments, team coordination.
