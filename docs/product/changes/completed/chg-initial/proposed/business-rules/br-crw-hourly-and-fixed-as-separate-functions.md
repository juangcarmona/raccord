## Rule

When the same role is sometimes charged hourly and sometimes at a fixed amount, two functions are kept that share the external name and differ in internal name, so customers see one name.

## Rationale

External names reach customer documents; internal names guide planners.

## Examples

Stagehand (hourly) and Stagehand (day) both appear as Stagehand on the quote.

## Exceptions

None.
