## Definition

The event that makes one part of an invoice moment due: after agreement (the project is confirmed), before the first day of the planning period, or after the last day of the planning period.

## Distinguish From

Issue date of the resulting invoice.

## Usage

Determines the due date of entries in the invoicing queue.
