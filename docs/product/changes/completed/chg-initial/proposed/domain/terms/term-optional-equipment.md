## Definition

A planned line flagged as an option offered to the customer (to choose between alternatives or as an extra). Its price is excluded from the quotation total; quotation layouts may list options among other lines or in a separate section.

## Distinguish From

Not cancelled equipment: the line remains planned.

## Usage

Used in proposals.
