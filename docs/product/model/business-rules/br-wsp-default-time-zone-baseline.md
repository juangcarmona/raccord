## Rule

The default time zone is derived from the company's location at set-up, applies to the whole installation when changed, and every new project starts in it.

## Rationale

A single reference avoids confusion when crew in other regions view the planning.

## Examples

- A crew member who views times in another zone still sees that the company plans in its default zone.

## Exceptions

None.
