## Definition

The original purchase price of a rental item at the moment it was first bought. It is used to value rental stock (list price times quantity).

## Distinguish From

- Purchase price: the equivalent field for sale items.
- Rental price: what the customer pays.

## Usage

Exported for stock valuation of rental items.
