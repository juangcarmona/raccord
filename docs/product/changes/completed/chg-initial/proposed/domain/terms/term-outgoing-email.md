## Definition

A message sent through the company's configured mail account to a mailbox: documents to customers and suppliers (quotations, contracts, invoices, packing slips, call sheets), planning and invitations to crew, and system messages. Attributes: sender address (TERM-SENDER-ADDRESS), recipients, subject (from the template subject, editable), message text (from an e-mail template, editable), the sender's signature, attachments, sending user, time sent and delivery status (TERM-EMAIL-DELIVERY-STATUS).

## Distinguish From

Communication record: the log entry for any outgoing communication, including in-app notifications; the e-mail is one channel.

## Usage

Composed after generating a document or produced automatically; tracked in the communication log.
