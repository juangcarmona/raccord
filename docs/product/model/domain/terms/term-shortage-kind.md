## Definition

The classification of the cause of a shortage, given to the planner as an explanation:

- **Stock shortage**: there is simply not enough stock available.
- **Unreserved own stock**: enough own stock is free, but it is not yet reserved for the line (for example after another project was cancelled).
- **Invalid reservation**: the line was reserved, but stock changed afterwards.
- **Temporary article**: the line plans an article that is not owned at all.

## Distinguish From

Not a lifecycle; a shortage may change kind as conditions change.

## Usage

Drives which resolution is offered and suggested.
