## Rule

A pie chart of a statistics view shows at most the ten largest results and groups all remaining results into one "other" slice.

## Rationale

Keeps charts readable.

## Examples

Twenty-five customers by revenue show ten slices plus "other".

## Exceptions

Bar charts are not limited.
