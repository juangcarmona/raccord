## Definition

A sellable item of a project that bundles task work (design, editing, pre- and post-production). Attributes: name and description shown to the customer, estimated time in hours, price, hourly cost rate, VAT class, debit and credit ledgers, discount percentage, details, service group and linked tasks. A service appears in its own section of quotes and invoices and has a breakdown of estimated, planned and actual cost, revenue, discount and profit. It may exist without tasks (then it has no hours).

## Distinguish From

Additional cost: a single bought-in line without hours. Crew function: a role at the event. Task: carries hours but cannot be invoiced alone.

## Usage

Pricing and invoicing of production work.
