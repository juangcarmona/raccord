## Definition

Derived state of a crew member or vehicle for a given function period: available (declared available or accepted an availability invitation), unavailable (overlapping assignment, blocking appointment, blocking shift, declared unavailable or declined invitation), invited (invitation or availability request awaiting answer), reserved (held by a crew reservation) or unknown (no information).

## Distinguish From

Availability entry: one declared fact; the state is the result of all facts. Equipment availability: stock-based.

## Usage

Colour-coded in all planning views to choose whom to plan.
