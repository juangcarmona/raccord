## Rule

The planned cost of an activity, which for a freelancer is the agreed total rate for the job, is shown only to users whose role grants it.

## Rationale

Rates are confidential.

## Examples

A freelancer with the right sees the agreed amount to invoice.

## Exceptions

None.
