## Rule

The usage period is the period charged to the customer: its length determines the price factor of the project, and statistics compute the average daily rental price over it.

## Rationale

Customers pay for the days they use, not the preparation days.

## Examples

Planning period five days, usage period two days: rental is priced over two days.

## Exceptions

None.
