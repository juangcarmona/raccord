## Definition

A display option of per-day, calendar, timeline and personal-schedule views that limits the days on which a project is shown to those within its planning period (default) or within its usage period.

## Distinguish From

Period filter: limits which projects are shown, not on which days.

## Usage

Showing actual event days on an office screen.
