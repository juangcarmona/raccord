## Definition

A block of internal work outside any project (warehouse, deliveries, office) on which crew members and vehicles are planned. Attributes: name, from and until, total break time, recurrence, whether it leaves people available for other functions, required quantity, planned crew and vehicles, remarks for planner and crew member, tags and custom fields. Shifts can be created from a default shift.

## Distinguish From

Function: project work. Appointment: personal commitment, not staffed.

## Usage

Planning internal work, inviting to it, registering hours against it.
