## Definition

A complete point-in-time copy of the installation's data taken automatically on the schedule the company configures and kept for the retention period the company configures. It can be used to restore the whole installation to that moment.

## Distinguish From

Not an export of selected records.

## Usage

Recovery from large-scale data damage such as a failed import.
