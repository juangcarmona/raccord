## Definition

An unnumbered, editable invoice prepared from unbilled lines of one project, for that project's customer, in one series.

## Distinguish From

Issued invoice: a draft becomes read-only once issued and the issued invoice is a separate, immutable record.

## Usage

Created, revised, discarded or issued by the billing administrator.
