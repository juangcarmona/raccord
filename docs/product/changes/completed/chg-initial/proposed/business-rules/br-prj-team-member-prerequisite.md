## Rule

Only crew members whose role allows seeing the task area can be added to a team or to a project task board; no further permission is needed to work on tasks of boards they can access.

## Rationale

Access must be usable.

## Examples

A crew member without task access is not offered as team member.

## Exceptions

None.
