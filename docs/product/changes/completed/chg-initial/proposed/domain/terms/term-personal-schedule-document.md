## Definition

A generated document, based on a template and letterhead, listing one crew member's planning over a chosen period; downloadable as PDF or word-processing file, sendable by email or printable.

## Distinguish From

Call sheet: whole-project planning for all crew and vehicles.

## Usage

Formal per-person schedule.
