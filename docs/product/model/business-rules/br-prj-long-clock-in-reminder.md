## Rule

A person clocked in for more than 12 hours receives reminder notifications in the product and by e-mail.

## Rationale

Forgotten timers inflate hours.

## Examples

See SB-PRJ-FORGOTTEN-TIMER-REMINDER.

## Exceptions

None.
