## Rule

The change history lists saves grouped per user in one-hour intervals, and per save the added, modified and deleted elements organised by area, group, item and field with old and new values.

## Rationale

Readable accountability.

## Examples

A quantity change shows area equipment, group Sound, item mixer, field quantity 2 to 3.

## Exceptions

None.
