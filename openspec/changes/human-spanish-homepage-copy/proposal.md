## Why

The Spanish homepage uses abstract phrases that do not tell audiovisual production teams what they can do in Raccord. Visitors also need an honest, plain warning about sign-in, public access and the product's current limits.

## What Changes

- Replace generic headings and calls to action with direct Spanish descriptions of the current customer, project, billable-work and invoice flow.
- Keep the language specific to production companies and equipment-rental businesses.
- Add an accessible GitHub mark with a visible text label.
- State that sign-in is required, there is no hosted demo or public registration, and legal or fiscal compliance is not claimed.
- Link to the maintained Docker Compose installation guide.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `public-capability-overview`: the Spanish page explains current workflows and limits in concrete terms.

## Product definition impact

The copy describes existing workflows and does not add product behavior or make a compliance claim.
