## Definition

Per-crew-member overview of past and current functions, appointments and availability by date: days with events are marked, the chosen day lists projects and appointments, and multi-day jobs show which day of the job it is (for example day 2 of 3).

## Distinguish From

Personal schedule: the person's own calendar view.

## Usage

Planner insight into one person's commitments.
