## Rule

When refreshing planned virtual combinations, the planner may also realign their content with the catalog definition; content lines no longer in the catalog definition are then removed from the project.

## Rationale

Long tours and duplicated projects must pick up kit redesigns.

## Examples

A DJ kit redefined without a second monitor loses that monitor line when realigned.

## Exceptions

None.
