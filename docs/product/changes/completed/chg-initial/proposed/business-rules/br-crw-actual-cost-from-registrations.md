## Rule

The actual crew cost of a project is the sum of hours registered against its functions priced at the rate of each assignment.

## Rationale

Compare estimate and outcome.

## Examples

Two crew registering 10 hours at 30 per hour add 600 actual cost.

## Exceptions

None.
