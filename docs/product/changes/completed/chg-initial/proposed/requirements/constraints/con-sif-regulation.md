## Constraint

As invoicing software, Raccord must guarantee integrity, conservation, accessibility, legibility, traceability and inalterability of billing records, and must be able to generate chained billing records and report them to the tax authority as specified by Real Decreto 1007/2023 and Orden HAC/1177/2024.

## Rationale

Imposed by Spanish regulation; adaptation deadlines in force are 2027-01-01 and 2027-07-01, with a postponement announced but not enacted on 2026-10-05.

## Consequences

Requires immutability, audit, a compliance record per invoice and, later, reporting. For Raccord's self-hosted model, each installing company is responsible for the installation it operates, signs its own responsible declaration and uses its own electronic certificate. Raccord provides the declaration template and release information; modified forks or customisations require their own declaration. This product boundary remains subject to legal verification before any compliance claim.
