## Definition

A project invoice covering less than the whole project: a percentage of the project sum, typically driven by an invoice moment. The share is recorded with the invoice.

## Distinguish From

Partial payment (a payment smaller than the outstanding amount of one invoice).

## Usage

Used for down payments and staged billing; later invoices deduct it.
