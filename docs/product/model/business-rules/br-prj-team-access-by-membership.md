## Rule

Every member of a team sees all tasks linked to the team and all its boards, including tasks created before joining; no one outside the team can be given access to a team board individually.

## Rationale

Access stays in sync with organisation.

## Examples

See SB-PRJ-NEW-TEAM-MEMBER-SEES-HISTORY.

## Exceptions

None.
