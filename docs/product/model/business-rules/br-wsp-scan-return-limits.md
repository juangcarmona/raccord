## Rule

Bulk articles can only be returned by scan return up to the quantity planned on a project, and excess scans must be stocked separately.

## Rationale

Over-returns indicate a mismatch.

## Examples

- Scanning 12 cables against a project with 10 planned prompts for how to stock the extra 2.

## Exceptions

None.
