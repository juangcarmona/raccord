## Definition

A per-crew-member setting deciding who maintains personal and administrative details. When the profile is self-maintained, the crew member enters their own information (photo, phone, personal data) and planners cannot change it; otherwise planners maintain it and the crew member cannot.

## Distinguish From

User role rights: govern modules and actions in general; profile ownership governs only who edits this person's details.

## Usage

Data quality and privacy of personal data.
