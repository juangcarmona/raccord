## Rule

The discount on the whole project is either a percentage of the total price or a fixed amount; typing a desired project total sets the discount to the difference.

## Rationale

Lets the seller hit a negotiated round figure.

## Examples

See SB-FIN-TYPED-TOTAL-BECOMES-DISCOUNT.

## Exceptions

None.
