## Definition

A commercial offer document generated from a project, copying the plan's prices at generation time, with number series and number, version, expiration date, template, letterhead, personal text, conditions and the customer. A project can hold several quotes (alternatives) and each quote several versions. It can be downloaded, e-mailed, published online and signed digitally.

## Distinguish From

Contract: generated and handled identically, differing only in name and purpose; invoice: a billing document, not an offer.

## Usage

Prepared by the operations coordinator before the job is confirmed.
