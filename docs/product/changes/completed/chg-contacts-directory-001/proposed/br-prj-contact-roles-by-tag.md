## Rule

A contact's roles such as customer and supplier are distinguished by tags; folders only store contacts and cannot group them across roles.

## Rationale

A contact can be both customer and supplier.

## Examples

A company tagged customer and supplier appears in both filters.

## Exceptions

None.
