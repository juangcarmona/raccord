## Rule

Users who may not edit a record can still upload files to it when their role allows it; users who may edit a record can always add notes and files.

## Rationale

Staff on the floor must document issues (receipts, photos) without being able to change the record.

## Examples

- A warehouse worker uploads a receipt to an item they cannot edit.

## Exceptions

None.
