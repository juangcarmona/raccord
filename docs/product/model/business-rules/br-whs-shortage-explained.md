## Rule

The product explains each shortage with its shortage kind (stock shortage, unreserved own stock, invalid reservation, temporary article), so the planner knows which resolution applies.

## Rationale

Different causes need different fixes; some need no extra equipment at all.

## Examples

A line short because a competing job was cancelled is explained as unreserved own stock and fixed by re-saving.

## Exceptions

None.
