## Rule

Invoices are submitted directly to the tax authority only after the company has provided its own valid digital certificate.

## Rationale

The submission is made under the taxpayer's own identity.

## Examples

Without the uploaded certificate, reporting cannot be switched on.

## Exceptions

None.
