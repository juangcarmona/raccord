## Definition

A named date marked for the whole organization (public holiday, company event), optionally repeating every year. It is highlighted in personal schedules, crew planning timelines, project details and timelines and the schedule widget; it never blocks any action.

## Distinguish From

Not leave or unavailability of a person; not an appointment.

## Usage

Planning awareness.
