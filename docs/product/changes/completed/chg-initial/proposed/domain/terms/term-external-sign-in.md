## Definition

Signing in to a personal profile through an external identity provider (a Microsoft or Google account) instead of a password. A profile may use an external sign-in, a password, or both; a profile without password can add one later.

## Distinguish From

Not enforced single sign-on, which is a company policy restricting who may sign in and how.

## Usage

Profile creation and sign-in.
