## Definition

A priced piece of project work: kind (equipment, labour, transport, consumable, service, other), description, quantity, unit price, discount and VAT rate, in the order it was recorded.

## Distinguish From

Invoice line: the copy of a billable line inside a draft or issued invoice.

## Usage

The unit of billing; it is billed at most once.
