## Rule

A private task is visible only to its creator, is always assigned to the creator and never appears on a shared board; tasks assigned to all or selected crew are visible to everyone with access.

## Rationale

Personal work stays personal.

## Examples

A private reminder never shows on the project board shared with the client.

## Exceptions

None.
