## Definition

The cost a project is expected to cause, computed from planned crew and transport functions (fixed cost plus hourly or per-kilometre cost, times quantity), purchase prices of sale equipment, crew expenses and additional costs, before people or vehicles are chosen.

## Distinguish From

Planned cost, which uses the actual people and vehicles chosen; actual cost, which uses what really happened.

## Usage

Used mainly while the project is still an inquiry or draft, to judge margin and possible discount.
