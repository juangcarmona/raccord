## Definition

The access side of a crew member in the installation: whether the person may log in, the email address used for login, the assigned user role, the login status and the last-seen moment. Every user is a crew member, but not every crew member may log in.

## Distinguish From

Not the crew-member record as a planning resource (functions, rates, availability). Not the personal profile, which holds the person's own information.

## Usage

Managed by users allowed to change login information; drives authentication and authorization in the installation.
