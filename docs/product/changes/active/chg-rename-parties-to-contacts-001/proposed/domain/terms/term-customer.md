## Definition

A company or person the issuer bills. It may be registered with only a legal name; it becomes invoiceable once its tax identifier and complete address are known.

## Distinguish From

Recipient: the customer's legal identity as frozen on one issued invoice.

## Usage

Projects belong to one customer.
