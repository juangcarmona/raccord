## Definition

The weight of a physical kit without any content (for example an empty flight case). The total weight of a physical kit is its empty weight plus the weight of its default content. Available only for physical kits.

## Distinguish From

- Item weight of a single item: the weight of the piece itself.
- Virtual kit weight: does not exist; the content's weights are used.

## Usage

Feeds project weight totals for transport planning.
