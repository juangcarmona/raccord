## Rule

A yes to an availability invitation marks the crew member available for the requested period and a no marks them unavailable; no assignment is created and no job details are shown in the invitation.

## Rationale

Asking before committing.

## Examples

A yes on 3 June makes the member available for that period.

## Exceptions

None.
