## Rule

Changing the grouping of a board affects only the user's own view unless it is saved to the board, in which case everyone sees it.

## Rationale

Personal preferences must not disturb colleagues.

## Examples

Grouping by assignee for oneself leaves colleagues' status columns intact.

## Exceptions

None.
