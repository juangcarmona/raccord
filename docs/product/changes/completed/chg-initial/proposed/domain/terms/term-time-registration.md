## Definition

A record of time of one or more crew members: from and until, break (total or periods), optional travel time, hour type, optional distance, lunch flag, activities, status and change history. It is captured manually, from planned activities or by clocking in and out.

## Distinguish From

Tracked task time: hours on tasks. Shift: planned internal work, not tracked task time.

## Usage

Actual cost, payroll export, reconciliation.
