## Definition

A named section inside a project's equipment plan used to separate planned lines for planning and documents, for example by discipline (audio, video, rigging), by area or stage, or by time period. A group carries a name, an optional remark that can be shown to the customer on the quotation, a default planning period and a default usage period (initially those of the project), or alternatively a duration. Catalog articles may name a default group into which they can be placed with one action.

## Distinguish From

Not a catalog folder: folders organise the catalog, groups organise one project's plan. Not a packing list: packing lists are cut by pickup and return date, regardless of group.

## Usage

Used to organise the plan, as the default source of times for the lines in it, as the unit that can be marked delayed in the warehouse, and as grouping on quotations and packing slips.
