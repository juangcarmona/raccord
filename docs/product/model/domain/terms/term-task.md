## Definition

A piece of work to be delivered by a deadline rather than at a fixed time. Attributes: name (the only mandatory field), deadline (none, fixed date, or relative to a project moment), task status and completion, priority (high, average, low, none), colour, details, assignees (nobody, all crew, or one or more crew members), visibility (shared or private), planned time, teams, subtasks, notes, files, tags, custom fields, linked record (project, contact, equipment, vehicle, quote, contract, invoice, and others) and at most one production service. Hours can be tracked against it.

## Distinguish From

Crew function: a role at the event with fixed start and end, shown in crew planning. Crew shift: internal work at a fixed time without a project. Production service: a sellable bundle of tasks. Subtask: a checklist item without owner or deadline.

## Usage

Pre- and post-production work, reminders, follow-ups; appears in task lists and boards, never in crew planning.
