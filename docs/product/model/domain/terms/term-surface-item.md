## Definition

An item priced by area (width times height) rather than per piece, such as stage floor or backdrop. The catalog stores a standard size and the price belonging to it; when the item is planned, the planner enters the required dimensions and the price is computed from them.

## Distinguish From

- Measuring unit: names the unit of quantity but does not change price calculation.

## Usage

Used for floors, screens, drapes and other area-based goods.
