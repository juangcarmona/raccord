# Logo usage

## Mark concept

The mark is made of two linked frame-like paths. The overlap represents continuity: separate pieces of a production becoming one reliable workflow.

## Clear space

Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least one quarter of its rendered width. Do not place it against visually noisy backgrounds.

## Approved early uses

- Full-color mark on white or cloud backgrounds.
- Full-color mark on ink backgrounds.
- Single-color ink mark on light backgrounds.
- Single-color white mark on dark backgrounds.

## Do not

- Stretch, rotate, or skew the mark.
- Add shadows, bevels, or gradients.
- Recolor it with arbitrary status colors.
- Add a camera, reel, clapperboard, or play icon.
- Treat the exploratory PNG as the final master asset.

## Future asset set

- Final SVG mark.
- Light and dark monochrome variants.
- Wordmark lockup.
- Favicon and app icon.
- Social/avatar crop.
- Exported PNGs at 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, and 512 pixels.
