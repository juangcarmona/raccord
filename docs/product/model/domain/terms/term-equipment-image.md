## Definition

A picture attached to an item, stored among its files. It can be found through a web image search, uploaded directly as the item image or added as a file. The first uploaded image that is not marked confidential becomes the default image. Images cannot be imported in bulk.

## Distinguish From

- Attached file: any document on the item (receipt, warranty); images are files with a display role.

## Usage

Shown in item details, and available to online rental shop listings and documents.
