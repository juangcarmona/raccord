## Rule

Deleting a crew member removes all their past data; to retire a person the product offers disabling login and planning instead, which keeps history.

## Rationale

Historical planning, hours and costs must remain reportable.

## Examples

A retired technician is hidden from planning and blocked from login instead of deleted.

## Exceptions

None.
