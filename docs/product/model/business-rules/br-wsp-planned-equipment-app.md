## Rule

The right to see planned equipment of projects in the schedule applies only to the mobile web app.

## Rationale

Packers consult what to load from their phones.

## Examples

- A warehouse worker with this right sees the equipment list of their projects in the mobile web app.

## Exceptions

None.
