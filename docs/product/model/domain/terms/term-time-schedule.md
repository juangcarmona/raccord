## Definition

The ordered list of named time periods of a project. Each schedule contains, by default, one period designated as planning period and one designated as usage period; any further periods (load-in, build, rehearsal, show, break, transport, cleaning, pickup and return) may be added. Periods can be sorted manually or chronologically, duplicated, shifted, extended or shortened. Equipment groups, crew functions and transport functions are linked to periods so that they follow period changes.

## Distinguish From

Personal schedule: a person's own planning across projects. Crew shift: internal work time that belongs to no project.

## Usage

Required before a regular project can be saved; drives availability, pricing, overview display and the dates printed on documents.
