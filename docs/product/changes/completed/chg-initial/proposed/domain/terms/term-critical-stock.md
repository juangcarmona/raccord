## Definition

A replenishment threshold on a tracked sale item: the stock level at which the company wants to reorder before running out. Items at or below their critical stock can be filtered in the catalog.

## Distinguish From

- Shortage: planned demand exceeds availability; critical stock is a reorder signal, not a shortage.

## Usage

Used to monitor consumables.
