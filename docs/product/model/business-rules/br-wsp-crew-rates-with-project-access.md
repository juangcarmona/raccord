## Rule

Users with access to the projects module always see crew members' rates in planned activities, regardless of the specific crew-rate visibility permission.

## Rationale

Rates are already visible through project financials.

## Examples

- Hiding crew rates in activities has no effect for a role with project access.

## Exceptions

None.
