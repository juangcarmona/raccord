## Rule

Every crew expense category carries a cost (paid) and a price (charged); the margin is shown as a percentage of the total and as an amount.

## Rationale

Expenses are often re-invoiced with or without a markup.

## Examples

Meals costing 40 and charged 50 show a 20% margin (10.00).

## Exceptions

None.
