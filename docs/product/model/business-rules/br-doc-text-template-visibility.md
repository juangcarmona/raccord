## Rule

When saving a text template, its author chooses for whom it is visible; only those users are offered it.

## Rationale

Personal phrasing and shared company phrasing coexist.

## Examples

- A coordinator keeps a personal invoice message private and shares a standard quotation message with everyone.

## Exceptions

The available visibility choices are not documented.
