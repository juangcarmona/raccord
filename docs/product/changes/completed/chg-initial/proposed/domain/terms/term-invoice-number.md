## Definition

The identifier allocated to an invoice at issue time, correlative within a series and calendar year, printed as series code, year, a hyphen and five digits (for example F2026-00001).

## Distinguish From

Draft identifier: drafts have no invoice number.

## Usage

Allocated only when an invoice is issued.
