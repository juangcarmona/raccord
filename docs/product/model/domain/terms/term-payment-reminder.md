## Definition

A document generated manually for an overdue invoice, from a reminder template and letterhead, optionally with a new due date. Reminders are counted per invoice (first, second, third) and the date of the latest one is recorded.

## Distinguish From

Automatic notification: reminders are never sent automatically.

## Usage

Sent to the customer to chase payment.
