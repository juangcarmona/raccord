## Definition

The identifier of a party for tax purposes (NIF in Spain), stored uppercase without spaces, dots or hyphens.

## Distinguish From

Not a VAT number format check; validation of check digits is not yet defined.

## Usage

Mandatory for the issuer and for the recipient of a full invoice.
