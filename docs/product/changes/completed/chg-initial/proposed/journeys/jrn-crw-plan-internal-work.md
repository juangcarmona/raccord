## Intended Outcome

Non-project work is staffed and its hours registered.

## Entry Conditions

Internal work is foreseen.

## Journey Narrative

The planner defines default shifts, creates recurring shifts, invites or plans crew and vehicles, and crew register hours against shifts.

## Variants and Branches

Shifts leave crew available for project functions, which are planned on top.

## Completion Conditions

Shifts are staffed and registered.
