## Definition

An outgoing e-mail whose acceptance the company's mail account confirmed.

## Distinguish From

Read or opened: not tracked; acceptance says nothing about whether the recipient saw the message or whether it was filed as spam.

## Usage

Used to tell customers when a document was sent.
