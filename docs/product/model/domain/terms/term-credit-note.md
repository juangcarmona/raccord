## Definition

In Raccord, the general idea of a negative or cancelling invoice is realised as a Spanish rectifying invoice (TERM-RECTIFYING-INVOICE): a new document in the rectifying series that references the invoice it corrects, by differences or by substitution. Refunding a deposit is recorded as a rectifying invoice plus a refund payment.

## Distinguish From

Rectifying invoice (TERM-RECTIFYING-INVOICE): the Spanish legal form of correction; a credit note is the general mechanism the documentation describes. Payment refund: recorded as a payment on the credit note.

## Usage

Corrects a finalized invoice or refunds a deposit.
