## Definition

A yes/no setting on an item stating whether it is offered when planning equipment on projects. A hidden item stays in the catalog and keeps its stock, but cannot be selected when planning; it can still be booked out in the warehouse as additional equipment.

## Distinguish From

- Archived: the item leaves stock entirely; hiding only removes it from planning choices.

## Usage

Recommended alternative to deletion for items no longer offered.
