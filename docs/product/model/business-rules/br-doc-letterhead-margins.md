## Rule

Generated document content is laid out only within the page area left free by the letterhead's margins.

## Rationale

Prevents content from running over the printed header, footer or side elements of the letterhead.

## Examples

- A letterhead whose header occupies the top 4 cm is configured with a 4 cm top margin, so the content starts below it.
- Margins that are too small make content overlap the letterhead artwork; the remedy is to enlarge them.

## Exceptions

None.
