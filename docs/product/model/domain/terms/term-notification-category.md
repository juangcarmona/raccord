## Definition

A family of events that can trigger notifications, each with its own channel choice:
- Invitations sent or answered.
- Project changes (for example status, customer or location).
- Financial updates (for example a quotation signed, an invoice paid).
- Tasks (assigned, deadline passed).
- Maintenance (equipment reported damaged or lost).
- Email (received, failed to send).
- Time registrations changed by somebody else.
- Important days falling in a chosen period.

## Distinguish From

Not the notification itself.

## Usage

Basis of notification preferences.
