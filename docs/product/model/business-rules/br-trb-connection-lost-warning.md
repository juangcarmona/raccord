## Rule

When the live update connection is lost, the product tells the user that the connection was lost, that colleagues' updates may have been missed, and recommends refreshing to load the latest data.

## Rationale

Users must not unknowingly act on outdated information.

## Examples

See SB-TRB-CONNECTION-LOST.

## Exceptions

None.
