## Definition

The venue of a project, chosen from the contact database. Once chosen, the product shows it on a map with the distance and travel time from the warehouse. Selecting it may add the contact's automatic project note and show its warning.

## Distinguish From

Warehouse: the company's own store that supplies the equipment.

## Usage

Logistics, call sheets, travel calculations.
