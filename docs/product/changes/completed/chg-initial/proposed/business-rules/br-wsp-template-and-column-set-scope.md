## Rule

Users may change or delete only their own email and text templates unless their role also allows shared ones; likewise they may create and delete only their own column sets unless allowed to manage shared ones.

## Rationale

Shared assets affect everyone.

## Examples

- A crew planner edits their personal email text but cannot delete the shared invitation text.

## Exceptions

None.
