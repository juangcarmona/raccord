## Definition

For each revenue category and in total, revenue minus cost (profit, as an amount) or the same difference expressed as a percentage of revenue (margin). Both views of the same figure are available.

## Distinguish From

Revenue alone; the margin of a single crew expense, which compares its own price and cost.

## Usage

Guides discount decisions and post-job evaluation.
