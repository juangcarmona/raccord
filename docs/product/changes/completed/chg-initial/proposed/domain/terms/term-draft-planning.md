## Definition

Plan status in which an assignment exists for the planner but is hidden from the crew member's calendar and schedule.

## Distinguish From

Crew reservation: the person is informed of a hold; with draft planning they see nothing.

## Usage

Tentative staffing while a job is being arranged.
