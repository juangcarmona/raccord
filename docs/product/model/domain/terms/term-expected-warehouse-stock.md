## Definition

For an inventory count, the quantity of an item (or physical kit) that should be physically present in the warehouse at the moment the count was created: the item's quantity minus the units that are content of physical kits or out on location for projects. Virtual kits have none because their content varies.

## Distinguish From

- Stock quantity: everything owned, wherever it is.
- Availability: a forward-looking planning figure.

## Usage

Compared with the counted quantity to produce a count result.
