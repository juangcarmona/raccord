## Definition

The part of a document that lists a project's additional costs (TERM-ADDITIONAL-COST). Each line shows quantity, sale price (unit price before quantity and discount), discount and price in project (the line amount after quantity and discount). Cost groups are hidden by default; when shown, each group shows its name and subtotal. A total of all additional costs, including discounts, closes the section.

## Distinguish From

Additional cost: the commercial line itself. Sale price versus price in project: only the latter always adds up to the totals.

## Usage

Placed on quotations, contracts and invoices.
