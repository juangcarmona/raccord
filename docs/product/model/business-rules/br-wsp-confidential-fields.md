## Rule

Custom fields marked confidential are only shown to users whose role allows seeing confidential fields.

## Rationale

Lets the company store sensitive attributes alongside regular data.

## Examples

- A salary-band field on crew members is hidden from the freelancer role.

## Exceptions

None.
