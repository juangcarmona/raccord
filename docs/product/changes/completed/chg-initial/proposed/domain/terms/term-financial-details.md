## Definition

Prices, costs, rates, margins, invoices and financial charts throughout the product (dashboard charts, warehouse and equipment prices, contact payment data, crew rates, vehicle costs, activity financials, project prices and the financial overview, statistics). Access to them is governed by the role.

## Distinguish From

Operational data (quantities, schedules), which stay visible.

## Usage

Hidden as a whole for roles without financial visibility.
