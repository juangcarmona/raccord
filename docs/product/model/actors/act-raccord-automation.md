## Purpose

Time-based and event-driven behaviour the product performs without a person: recalculating availability, sending reminders and notifications, flagging overdue items.

## Goals

Keep derived state current and people informed.

## Responsibilities

Runs scheduled and triggered jobs within the rules of each area.

## Boundaries

Never makes financial or commercial decisions that require human approval.
