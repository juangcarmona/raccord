## Constraint

The product never deletes crew documents automatically; their retention and removal are the company's responsibility under applicable data protection law.

## Rationale

Personal data regulation obliges the controller to decide retention.

## Consequences

Companies must remove expired personal documents themselves; the product must allow manual removal.
