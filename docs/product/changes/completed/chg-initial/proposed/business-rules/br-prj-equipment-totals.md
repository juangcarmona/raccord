## Rule

The product totals weight, volume, power usage, power, rental price and discounted rental price of planned equipment at equipment group and project level, using the values recorded on items.

## Rationale

Logistics and power planning.

## Examples

Two 20 kg items in a group: group weight 40 kg.

## Exceptions

Items without recorded values add nothing.
