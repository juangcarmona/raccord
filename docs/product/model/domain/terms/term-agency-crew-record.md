## Definition

A crew member record that represents an employment agency or hiring company rather than one person. It is flagged as plannable several times at the same moment, so many hired people can be planned under the same record without creating double-booking conflicts.

## Distinguish From

Ordinary crew member: one person, at most one assignment at a time without conflict.

## Usage

Planning hired crew whose individual identities are not tracked.
