## Definition

A sales-tax rate (used in tax systems where rates depend on federal, state and local level) linked to a postal code, kept up to date automatically each month with a history of earlier rates. Equipment is marked as taxable or not taxable; customers can carry a tax rate.

## Distinguish From

VAT scheme per class.

## Usage

Used by accounts in such tax systems instead of VAT classes.
