## Rule

A folder marked confidential makes all its contents confidential and is visible only to users allowed to download all files; non-confidential folders may contain confidential and non-confidential items.

## Rationale

Protects contracts and personal data.

## Examples

See SB-PRJ-CONFIDENTIAL-FOLDER-HIDDEN.

## Exceptions

None.
