## Definition

Repetition definition for appointments, availability entries and shifts: one time (default), every N days, every N weeks on selected weekdays, every N months, every N years (each with an end date), or a free list of specific dates.

## Distinguish From

Default shift: a template, not a repetition.

## Usage

Creating series of items in one action; editing or deleting a series by scope.
