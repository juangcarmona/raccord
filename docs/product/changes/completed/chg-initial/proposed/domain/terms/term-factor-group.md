## Definition

A named table mapping ranges of rental days (from, to) to a price factor. One factor group is the default; equipment items can be assigned another group so that different kinds of equipment follow different tables.

## Distinguish From

Price factor: one value in the table.

## Usage

Configured in the financial settings and assigned to equipment items.
