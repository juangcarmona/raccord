## Rule

A yes to a draft-planning invitation plans the crew member on the function in draft; when more people accept than needed, all stay in draft and the planner chooses among them.

## Rationale

Lets the planner pick from willing crew.

## Examples

Four accept a function of quantity two; the planner keeps two.

## Exceptions

None.
