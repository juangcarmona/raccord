## Rule

In per-day, calendar, timeline and personal-schedule views a project is shown on all days of its planning period by default, or only on days within its usage period when that scope is chosen.

## Rationale

Shows real event days.

## Examples

See SB-PRJ-USAGE-DAY-SCOPE.

## Exceptions

None.
