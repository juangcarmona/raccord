## Purpose

Receives the billing records that Spanish regulation requires invoicing software to report.

## Goals

Receive complete, chained, unaltered billing records for every issued invoice.

## Responsibilities

Accepts, accepts with errors or rejects submitted records and returns evidence of receipt.

## Boundaries

Never changes Raccord's records; corrections are new records sent by Raccord.
