## Rule

The communication log includes every outgoing communication of the installation, whoever sent it and on any channel, including in-app communications between users such as notices between users; the sent e-mails view is limited to e-mails.

## Rationale

Gives a single place to answer whether someone was informed.

## Examples

- A notice shown to a crew member in the app appears in the log as an in-app communication but not among sent e-mails.

## Exceptions

Filtering by project is not available.
