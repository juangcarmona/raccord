## Definition

A single-purpose link sent to an email address to prove control of it, used to create a personal profile or to confirm a changed email address.

States: Sent (valid), Used, Expired (24 hours after sending). An expired link cannot be used; requesting verification again sends a fresh link.

## Distinguish From

Not the account invitation email, which a planner sends to a new crew member, although it leads to the same verification step.

## Usage

Email verification and profile email change.
