## Definition

Who can see a task: assigned to all crew members (everyone sees it), assigned to selected crew members (everyone sees it, the selected people are responsible), or private (only the creator sees it, it is always assigned to the creator and never appears on shared boards).

## Distinguish From

Board sharing: grants access to a set of tasks.

## Usage

Privacy of internal work; calendar sync scope.
