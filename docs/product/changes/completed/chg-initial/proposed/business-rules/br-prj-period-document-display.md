## Rule

Each time period carries separate settings for display on internal documents and on external documents; a period is printed only where enabled.

## Rationale

Internal periods such as packing must not appear on the customer's quote.

## Examples

Cleaning is shown on the packing slip but hidden on the quotation.

## Exceptions

None.
