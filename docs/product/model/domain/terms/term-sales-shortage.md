## Definition

A projected moment at which the stock of a sale (consumable) article would not cover the quantities planned for sale, computed from current sale stock and future plans. It shows when the article becomes short, and its stock at a date, and is resolved by scheduling a stock increase.

## Distinguish From

Not a rental shortage: sale stock decreases only on warehouse booking, not on planning.

## Usage

Listed separately from rental shortages for a period.
