## Rule

Any period of a schedule may be designated as the default planning period, the default usage period or both; groups and functions must then be linked to the intended periods.

## Rationale

Real jobs do not always fit the two generated periods.

## Examples

A 'show days' period is designated usage period instead of the generated one.

## Exceptions

None.
