## Definition

Per-assignment choice of how the function's travel time applies to a planned crew member: no transport, round trip, only the way there, only the way back. The function's travel time before and after can be taken over from the project location's distance.

## Distinguish From

Travel time in a time registration: time actually spent travelling, excluded from worked hours.

## Usage

Communicated call times include travel; deciding who drives in a vehicle.
