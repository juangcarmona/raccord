## Definition

A published, password-protectable web page presenting a quote with a title, welcome message and optional media, reachable by link. The customer can view, download and sign it and request changes; the company sees how often it was viewed and the change requests, and can take it offline or republish it.

## Distinguish From

Quote PDF sent as attachment; customer portal (not documented here).

## Usage

Optional alternative to sending the PDF.
