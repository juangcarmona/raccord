## Definition

A filtered list of crew members or vehicles used to pick resources for a selected function. Built-in lists: planned in this project, available at the function's time (reserved first, then available, then unknown) and matching tags. Custom lists are named and described, and gather members by individual pick, by record folder (including later additions) or by tags; they are shared in the workspace and each user can hide lists from their own view.

## Distinguish From

Saved view or filter preset: affects an overview; crew lists drive resource suggestions.

## Usage

Fast and informed staffing.
