## Responsibility

What the company rents, sells and owns: catalog items, kits, accessories, serial-numbered units, stock levels and availability over time.

## Language

Items, kits, accessories, alternatives, quantities, stock, stock counts, availability, shortages and reservations.

## Boundaries

## External Relationships
