## Definition

A party record in the company's contact database: a company or a private individual that acts as customer (client), venue (location), supplier, or several of these. Attributes: type, folder, contact number, name and identity details, visiting, mailing and billing addresses, distance and travel time, warning, automatic project note (subject and text), contact persons with default and administrative designations, commercial defaults (invoice moment, payment term, VAT scheme, specific insurance percentage, discount groups for rental and sale), tags, custom fields, notes, files, tasks and a change history.

## Distinguish From

Customer: the role of a contact chosen as client of projects and billed. Contact person: an individual affiliated with a contact. Crew member: a worker record, not a party.

## Usage

Clients and locations of projects, suppliers, CRM.
