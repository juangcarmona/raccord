## Rule

The warehouse board shows, for the selected date (today by default), packing lists of confirmed projects whose due date matches, plus lists on location, expected back and delayed.

## Rationale

The crew works by day.

## Examples

Changing the date to tomorrow shows tomorrow's pickups.

## Exceptions

None.
