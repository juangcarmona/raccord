## Definition

The customer has agreed to the project. Entered automatically when the customer digitally signs a quotation, or set manually. From this state the project's equipment becomes bookable in the warehouse and the project becomes eligible for invoicing.

## Distinguish From

In progress: warehouse handling of the project's equipment has started.

## Usage

Confirmed group; base of 'to be planned' and invoicing views.
