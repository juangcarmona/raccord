## Constraint

Invoices must contain the content required by Real Decreto 1619/2012 for full invoices, including correlative numbering within series, issue date, operation date when different, identification and tax identifiers of issuer and recipient, description, taxable base, rate and quota per rate, and total.

## Rationale

Imposed by Spanish law for issuers established in Spain, the first market.

## Consequences

Drives issue readiness, numbering and operation-date rules.
