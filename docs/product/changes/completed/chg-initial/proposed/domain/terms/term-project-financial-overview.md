## Definition

The per-project summary that sets, for each revenue category (rental equipment, sale equipment, crew, transport, additional costs and crew expenses), the estimated, planned and actual costs next to the revenue, the discount, the profit or margin and the total. It also carries the project-level discount, VAT scheme, insurance percentage, refundable deposit, invoice moment, conditions, price lock, the amount already invoiced and the quotes, contracts and invoices of the project.

## Distinguish From

Invoice: the overview is live and changes with the plan; invoices and quotes are generated from it at a moment in time. Statistics view: the overview covers one project, statistics aggregate many.

## Usage

Consulted while quoting to judge the room for discount and before and after the job to compare budget and outcome; starting point for generating financial documents.
