## Definition

A named group of crew members who work together (design, workshop, office, warehouse). Tasks can be linked to one or more teams; all members see all tasks and boards of their teams, including work created before they joined. A crew member may be in several teams and must be able to access the task area to be a member. Creating and editing teams is an administrator action.

## Distinguish From

Assignee: responsibility for a task; linking a task to a team does not assign it. Function group: crew roles inside a project.

## Usage

Access control and grouping for task work.
