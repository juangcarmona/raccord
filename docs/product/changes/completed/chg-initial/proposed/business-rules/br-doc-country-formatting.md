## Rule

Dates, numbers and amounts in a generated document are formatted according to the country of the template used, with the euro symbol.

## Rationale

Customers expect documents in their own regional conventions.

## Examples

See SB-DOC-COUNTRY-DATE-FORMAT.

## Exceptions

None.
