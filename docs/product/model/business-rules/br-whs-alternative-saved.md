## Rule

An article searched and used as alternative while solving a shortage is saved as an alternative of the short article for future shortages, even if none was configured before.

## Rationale

Knowledge of substitutes accumulates through use.

## Examples

The first time a wireless microphone model is substituted, it becomes a listed alternative.

## Exceptions

None.
