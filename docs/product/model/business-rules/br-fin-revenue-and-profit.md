## Rule

The revenue of each category is the sum of the totals of its planned content after discounts; profit is revenue minus cost and margin is profit as a percentage of revenue, per category and for the project.

## Rationale

One consistent definition makes projects comparable.

## Examples

Crew revenue 1,000 and crew cost 700 give a crew profit of 300 and a margin of 30%.

## Exceptions

None.
