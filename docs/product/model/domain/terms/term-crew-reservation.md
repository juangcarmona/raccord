## Definition

A hold on a crew member or vehicle for a time period, made by a planner or by acceptance of a reservation invitation, without disclosing a project or function. The crew member sees the reservation in their schedule; it may also be shown on a project's timeline.

## Distinguish From

Equipment reservation: secures stock. Draft planning: a hidden assignment on a specific function.

## Usage

Securing enough people or vehicles in busy periods.
