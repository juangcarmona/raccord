## Purpose

Turns recorded project work into correct, numbered invoices and keeps the issuer's legal details current.

## Goals

- Issue correct invoices the first time, with no gaps in numbering.
- Never edit an issued invoice; correct with new documents.
- Know before issuing what is missing.

## Responsibilities

- Defines the issuer profile and registers customers.
- Prepares invoice drafts from unbilled work, revises them and issues them.
- Obtains invoice documents and reviews the audit trail.

## Boundaries

Does not decide tax law; relies on the product to enforce the invoicing rules it implements.
