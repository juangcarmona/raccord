## Definition

A finalized invoice with its number, issue date, operation date, issuer and recipient snapshots, lines, VAT breakdown and totals, which never changes after issue.

## Distinguish From

Invoice draft; invoice document (a rendering of the issued invoice).

## Usage

Corrections are made with new documents, never by editing.
