## Rule

The total sale price of an additional cost is quantity times unit sale price reduced by its discount; its cost is quantity times purchase price per unit.

## Rationale

Costs passed on to customers keep their own margin.

## Examples

Three hotel nights at 120.00 with 10% discount total 324.00.

## Exceptions

None.
