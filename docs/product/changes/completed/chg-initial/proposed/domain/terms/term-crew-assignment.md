## Definition

The planning of one crew member or vehicle on one function or shift. Attributes: the resource, the function or shift, plan status (draft or final), crew planning period, applied crew rate, travel option, project-leader flag (internal reference, printable on call sheets) and a remark communicated to the crew member.

## Distinguish From

Invitation: an offer that may lead to an assignment. Crew reservation: a hold on a person without a function.

## Usage

Basis of personal schedules, call sheets, planned cost and time registration activities.
