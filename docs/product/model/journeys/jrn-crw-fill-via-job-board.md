## Intended Outcome

Open functions are filled by volunteers.

## Entry Conditions

The project has functions.

## Journey Narrative

The planner posts functions, crew sign up, the planner checks for conflicts the board does not prevent and shares schedules.

## Variants and Branches

Postings are removed when no longer needed.

## Completion Conditions

Posted functions are staffed.
