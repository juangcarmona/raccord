## Definition

Price rate: what the customer is charged for a function. Cost rate: what the work costs the company. Cost rates apply to functions and crew members; price rates only to functions.

## Distinguish From

Margin: the difference between price and cost, not a rate kind.

## Usage

Choosing rates for function price and cost.
