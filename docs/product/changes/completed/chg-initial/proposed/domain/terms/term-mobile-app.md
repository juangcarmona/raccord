## Definition

The responsive, installable version of the product for phones and tablets, used in the browser or added to the home screen. It lets people check their schedule, create appointments, enter availability, answer invitations, book equipment in and out by scanning codes with the camera or manually (with extra equipment and alternatives), return equipment by scanning, view projects, equipment and contacts and add files, notes and tasks, and register worked hours manually or by clocking in and out. It has its own language and theme settings.

## Distinguish From

Not a separate native application.

## Usage

Daily use by crew and warehouse staff.
