## Definition

Fixed classification of crew documents into five categories: contract (employment or freelance agreement), licence (permission to operate equipment or vehicles), certificate (training or qualification), insurance (proof of cover) and identity document (passport or national identity card).

## Distinguish From

Hour type: classifies time, not documents.

## Usage

Grouping documents per crew member; deriving whether a contract is active.
