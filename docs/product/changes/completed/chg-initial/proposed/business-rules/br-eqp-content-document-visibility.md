## Rule

Kit content is grouped under its kit on documents; each content line is shown or hidden on customer documents and on packing lists according to its own settings, unless the document being generated is set to show all or hide all content.

## Rationale

Customers may not need every cable listed; the crew does.

## Examples

- Content hidden on quotes still appears on the packing list when shown there.

## Exceptions

None.
