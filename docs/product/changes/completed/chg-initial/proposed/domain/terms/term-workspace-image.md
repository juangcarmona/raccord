## Definition

A picture uploaded by the workspace, shown as background of the sign-in page and on pages customers use to sign quotations and pay invoices; a 3:2 proportion fits best.

## Distinguish From

Not the document letterhead or logo printed on documents.

## Usage

Branding of customer-facing and sign-in pages.
