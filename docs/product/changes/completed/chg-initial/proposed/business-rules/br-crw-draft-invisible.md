## Rule

An assignment in draft is not shown in the crew member's calendar or schedule; only final assignments are.

## Rationale

Planners can rework without confusing crew.

## Examples

See SB-CRW-DRAFT-HIDDEN-FROM-CREW.

## Exceptions

None.
