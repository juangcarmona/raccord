## Definition

The number of pieces of an item that travel together in one package (for example glasses in crates of 10). The transport volume is entered for a full package, and the volume of a planned quantity is the number of packages needed (planned quantity divided by packed-per, rounded up) times that volume. It has no effect on stock or availability.

## Distinguish From

- Measuring unit: the unit in which quantity is expressed.
- Kit content quantity: defines what a kit consists of.

## Usage

Used only in transport volume calculation.
