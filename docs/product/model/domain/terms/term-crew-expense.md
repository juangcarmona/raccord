## Definition

An ancillary cost of crew work beyond the labour rate, in one of four fixed categories: catering (meals), travel, accommodation (lodging) and other (per diems, special conditions). Each has a cost (what the company pays) and a price (what it charges), giving a margin. It is defined on default functions, inherited by planned functions and can be overridden per planned crew member.

## Distinguish From

Additional cost (not tied to a function or person); crew rate (the labour cost itself).

## Usage

Shown in the financial overview breakdown.
