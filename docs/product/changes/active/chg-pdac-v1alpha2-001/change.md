## Problem

The accepted model records 731 business-rule-to-use-case governance relationships in business rules' `applies-to` selectors. PDaC v1alpha2 assigns these relationships to use cases' `governed-by` selectors. The current representation cannot be validated under v1alpha2, which the updated Lighthouse foundation requires.

## Intended Product Outcome

The same 794 distinct rule-to-use-case pairs represented across both current selector directions remain in the accepted Product Definition. Business rules retain their journey and bounded-context scopes. Use cases carry the rule IDs that govern them. Artifact IDs, status, titles, body text, and other relationships keep their existing meaning.

## Rationale

The serialization contract needs one unambiguous owner for each relationship. Keeping every pair intact lets Raccord adopt the current ProductShape contract without accepting or changing any product behavior.

## Affected Product Areas

Existing rules and use cases across the current product model. The overlay modifies 516 business rules and 250 use cases.

## Open Questions

None.

## Product Acceptance

The 731 legacy business-rule selectors and all existing use-case selectors form 794 distinct governance pairs. The proposed use-case selectors represent exactly those 794 pairs. No artifact body or unrelated metadata changes. The complete overlay validates under the current v1alpha1 provider contract before application; the resulting baseline must validate under v1alpha2 before the Lighthouse upgrade is considered complete.

## Out of Scope

No new behavior, rule, use case, legal claim, implementation, or runtime deployment is proposed. The Lighthouse and GitHub workflow updates remain separate repository tooling changes.
