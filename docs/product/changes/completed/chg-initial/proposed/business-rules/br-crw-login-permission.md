## Rule

When a crew member's login permission is off, the person cannot log in; the record and all its history remain.

## Rationale

People who leave the company keep their past planning and hours in reports.

## Examples

A freelancer who stopped working with the company is switched off and can no longer log in, while past projects still show them.

## Exceptions

None.
