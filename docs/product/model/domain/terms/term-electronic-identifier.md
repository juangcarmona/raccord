## Definition

The pair identification number and identification scheme (country/registry code) that identifies the company or a customer in e-invoicing.

## Distinguish From

Tax identifier (TERM-TAX-ID), which may be used as the number in some schemes.

## Usage

Mandatory for e-invoices.
