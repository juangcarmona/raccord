## Rule

When a crew member's profile is self-maintained, only the crew member can edit their personal details and planners cannot; otherwise only planners can edit them and the crew member cannot.

## Rationale

Avoids conflicting edits and lets people own their personal data.

## Examples

Phone number of a self-maintained profile is read-only for the planner.

## Exceptions

None.
