## Rule

Selecting one or more periods and requesting crew and transport functions creates one function per selected period, linked to it.

## Rationale

Speeds up crew planning set-up.

## Examples

Selecting build and break creates two functions linked to them.

## Exceptions

Requires crew planning capability.
