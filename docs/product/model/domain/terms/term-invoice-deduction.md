## Definition

A reference from a new invoice or credit note to an earlier invoice whose amount is subtracted from the new total, so that staged invoices of one project add up to the project price.

## Distinguish From

Credit note (a negative document); extra negative line (free amount, not linked to an invoice).

## Usage

Used on the final invoice after down payments and when crediting.
