## Definition

An invoice in a structured, machine-readable format (XML) carrying the parties' electronic identifiers, tax categories and bank account.

## Distinguish From

Invoice document as PDF or Word, which is not an e-invoice.

## Usage

Exported or attached to e-mails automatically.
