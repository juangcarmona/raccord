## Definition

A unique machine-readable identifier (QR code or barcode, at most 25 characters) attached to a catalog article or to a physical combination. All pieces of an article share one code. Codes are either generated by the product or assigned from pre-printed labels; several codes may be attached to the same article.

## Distinguish From

Not the equipment code, which is a short identifier printed on documents.

## Usage

Scanned to book, to look up an article, to report losses.
