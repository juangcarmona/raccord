## Definition

A read-only field the product computes rather than stores, such as estimated, planned and actual cost of a project, the equipment period of a project, totals of a planned function, or the parent path of a file. In collections it is left out unless explicitly selected.

## Distinguish From

Not a custom field, which users fill in.

## Usage

Exposed on projects, project functions and crew, files and invoice lines.
