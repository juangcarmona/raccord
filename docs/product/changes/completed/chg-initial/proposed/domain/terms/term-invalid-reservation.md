## Definition

A reserved quantity that can no longer be honoured because the stock behind it changed after reservation: units lost, a delayed return extending another project's hold, or units booked out for another project. Every project reserving that article in the affected interval may show an invalid reservation shortage.

## Distinguish From

Not a plain shortage (the plan was never covered); here the plan was covered and lost its cover.

## Usage

A shortage kind; resolved by reallocating reservations or restoring stock.
