## Definition

A set of signals on each time registration helping approvers: registered versus scheduled hours, differences between crew members on the same activity, presence and kind of linked activity, and registered time versus minimum paid hours. Each signal is graded aligned, small difference (under 30 minutes), large difference (over 30 minutes) or not applicable.

## Distinguish From

Reconciliation status: per activity and ungraded.

## Usage

Directing approver attention.
