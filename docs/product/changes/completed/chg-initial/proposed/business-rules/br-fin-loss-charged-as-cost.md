## Rule

When equipment returned from a project is marked lost or defective, a corresponding additional cost is added to that project so it can be charged to the customer.

## Rationale

Damage is billed through the normal financial flow.

## Examples

A microphone booked back as lost appears as an additional cost of the project.

## Exceptions

None.
