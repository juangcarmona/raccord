## Definition

The workspace's description of the company that uses it: name, address and country or region (which also determines regional defaults), contact and invoicing details, logo, and extras such as the workspace image.

## Distinguish From

Not the issuer profile used on invoices, although both describe the same organization; not customer contacts.

## Usage

Configured at onboarding; required before going live.
