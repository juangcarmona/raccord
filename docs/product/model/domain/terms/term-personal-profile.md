## Definition

The person's sign-in identity, keyed by one verified email address and protected by a password and/or an external sign-in (a Microsoft or Google account). It also holds the person's own information (personal details, qualifications, skills, travel details and documents). A profile is created when a crew member with a valid email address is allowed to sign in and accepts the invitation.

## Distinguish From

Not the crew-member record, which is the company's record of the person as a planning resource.

## Usage

Signing in and profile maintenance.
