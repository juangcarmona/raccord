## Rule

Once created, a custom field's definition cannot be edited except for the options of a drop-down field.

## Rationale

Existing records and documents rely on it.

## Examples

Changing a yes/no field to text is impossible; adding a drop-down option is allowed.

## Exceptions

Confidentiality can be changed later.
