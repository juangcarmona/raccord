## Definition

The period of a function that matters to the customer and, when quoting is used, the period charged for it.

## Distinguish From

Crew planning period: when the crew member is occupied; may be wider and drives costs, not price. Equipment usage period: the same idea for equipment lines.

## Usage

Price calculation of functions.
