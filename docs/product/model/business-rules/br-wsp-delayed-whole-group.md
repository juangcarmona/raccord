## Rule

Marking equipment as delayed applies to the entire equipment group of the project, not only to the missing items.

## Rationale

Documented limitation that can confuse partial returns.

## Examples

- Two missing receivers in a group of ten make the whole group show as delayed.

## Exceptions

None.
