## Rule

Imported items are planned in a single group dedicated to imported equipment, and their accessories are not planned automatically.

## Rationale

The import does not know the intended group structure.

## Examples

See SB-PRJ-IMPORT-NO-ACCESSORIES.

## Exceptions

None.
