## Intended Outcome

The right equipment leaves on time and its status is known.

## Entry Conditions

Packing lists are ready to pack and due.

## Journey Narrative

The technician opens the day's board, prints the slip, packs cases, books units by scan to prepped and then on location, adding extras or swapping alternatives as needed.

## Variants and Branches

Manual booking without scanning.

## Completion Conditions

All lines of the outgoing list are on location; the project shows in progress.
