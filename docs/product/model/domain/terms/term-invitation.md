## Definition

An offer or question sent to a crew member about a function, a shift or a time period. Attributes: invitation type, recipient, target (functions, shift or period), optional due date and reminder, message, salutation and greeting, information shown (due date, location, client name, function group, project leader, account manager), status and response.

## Distinguish From

Account invitation: invitation to log in. Job board posting: an open offer anyone may take.

## Usage

Gathering availability and filling functions.
