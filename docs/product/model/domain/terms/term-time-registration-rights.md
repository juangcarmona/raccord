## Definition

Role-based permissions for time registration: view, edit or remove own hours; view, edit or remove other crew members' hours; pre-register hours before working them; which activities are visible; clocking in from the mobile web app; plus the company limit of days back within which own hours can still be viewed or changed.

## Distinguish From

Crew profile ownership: personal data editing.

## Usage

Who registers whose hours.
