## Definition

A way of covering a shortage. Available kinds: replace with an alternative article; reallocation of reservations between projects. Each resolution is offered as usable or not usable for the selected shortage, depending on configuration (for example whether alternatives are defined).

## Distinguish From

Not a change of plan intent: resolutions keep the project's demand and change how it is covered (except replacing by alternative, which changes the article).

## Usage

Offered from the project plan and from the cross-project shortage list.
