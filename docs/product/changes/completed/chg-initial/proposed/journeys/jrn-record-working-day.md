## Intended Outcome

Each working day is recorded correctly with no extra paperwork.

## Entry Conditions

The employee has an account and a phone or office computer.

## Journey Narrative

The employee clocks in on arrival or at the job site, takes breaks, and clocks out. Mistakes are corrected with a reason and a manager's approval. Employees can always see their records, and the company can hand them to the inspectorate.

## Variants and Branches

Forgotten clock-out leaves the day open until corrected.

## Completion Conditions

Every day of the period is Finished or corrected.
