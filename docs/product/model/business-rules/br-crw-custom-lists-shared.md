## Rule

Custom crew and vehicle lists are visible to everyone in the company; editing or deleting one affects all users, whereas hiding a list affects only the user who hides it.

## Rationale

Shared planning vocabulary with personal views.

## Examples

Deleting the Riggers north list removes it for all planners.

## Exceptions

None.
