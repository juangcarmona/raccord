## Rule

In calendar and timeline views a project's colour is its own colour or its type's default colour; status is shown by styling, not by colour.

## Rationale

Avoids confusing type with stage.

## Examples

A blue festival project that is cancelled stays blue but struck through.

## Exceptions

None.
