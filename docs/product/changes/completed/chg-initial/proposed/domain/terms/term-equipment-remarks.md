## Definition

Two free-text notes on an item with different audiences: the external remark is printed on customer documents (quotes, contracts, invoices); the internal remark is printed on packing slips for the warehouse crew.

## Distinguish From

- Notes: internal comments on the item record that never print.
- Kit content document visibility: controls whether content lines print, not free text.

## Usage

Copied with the item onto planned lines and documents.
