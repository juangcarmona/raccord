## Definition

Part of a time registration describing the task performed and its duration: either linked to a planned function or shift, or a free-text manual activity. A registration may have several. Time in a registration not covered by activities is hours without activities.

## Distinguish From

Planned activity: the schedule side.

## Usage

Linking hours to projects and costs.
