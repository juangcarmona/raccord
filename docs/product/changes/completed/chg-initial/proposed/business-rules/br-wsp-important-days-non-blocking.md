## Rule

Important days are marked in schedules and timelines and users are notified when a selected period contains one, but no planning or other action is blocked on those dates; a day can repeat every year.

## Rationale

Awareness without constraint.

## Examples

- Planning a function on a public holiday is allowed and the day is visibly marked.

## Exceptions

None.
