## Rule

With the before-and-during setting, a planned person sees the location's contact details until the project ends and no longer afterwards.

## Rationale

Freelancers need venue contacts to do the job but not to approach the venue later.

## Examples

- The day after a festival ends, freelancers no longer see the venue contact.

## Exceptions

None.
