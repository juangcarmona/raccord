## Definition

A free description of where an item is kept inside the warehouse (aisle, shelf, bin). It is recorded per item, may be left empty, and can be shown in the catalog, in the warehouse and on packing slips.

## Distinguish From

- Equipment folder: organises the catalog, not the physical shelves.

## Usage

Helps the crew find equipment when packing.
