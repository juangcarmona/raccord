## Constraint

Raccord must not claim compliance with VERI*FACTU, e-invoicing or any tax regulation until the implementation has been verified against official specifications and reviewed by tax and legal experts.

## Rationale

Self-imposed: a false claim harms users legally.

## Consequences

Product text speaks of readiness, never of compliance.
