## Problem

Raccord has no accepted Product Definition. A small Spanish audiovisual rental or production company needs one internal tool for its equipment, calendars, people, legal clock-in record, quotes and invoices, which it can install and configure for itself. Today that scope exists only as research notes and a first implemented billing slice, so it cannot be validated, cited, planned or evolved through reviewed changes.

## Intended Product Outcome

The accepted definition describes Raccord as an internal, all-in-one toolkit for one company per installation (CON-SELF-HOSTABLE). It covers ten bounded contexts: Parties, Projects, Commercial, Invoicing, Equipment, Warehouse, Crew and transport, Documents and communication, Workspace, and Compliance and audit. Each has actors, journeys, use cases, business rules, domain terms with complete status lifecycles, structured behaviours, and traceable requirements and constraints. Core capabilities:

- **Inventory:** equipment catalog, kits and accessories, stock quantities, availability, shortages and reservations, packing lists, booking out and back manually or by QR/barcode, stock counts of selected items or all items, and loss reporting.
- **Projects and calendars:** projects with a required customer, a planning period and a usage period; project statuses, types and templates; internal requests; task boards; custom fields and saved views; and private calendar feeds.
- **People:** crew records, functions, availability, invitations, planning and conflicts, project hours, shifts, crew documents with signature, payroll file export, an internal job board, vehicles, and the legal workday record (JRN-RECORD-WORKING-DAY, FR-WORKDAY-RECORD).
- **Quotes and invoices:** quotes as a PDF and as an online page with a signature that confirms the project; invoices from selected lines or from advances that are deducted automatically (BR-INVOICE-BASIS); rectifying invoices by differences or by substitution (BR-RECTIFYING-INVOICE); payment tracking, reminders and deposits; EUR only; frozen issued PDFs; and an accounting file export.
- **Spanish compliance:** VERI*FACTU records submitted directly with the company certificate (BR-VERIFACTU-DIRECT-SUBMISSION), and B2B e-invoicing with status reporting (FR-B2B-E-INVOICING).
- **Installation:** four predefined, editable roles (Admin, Office, Warehouse, Crew); local sign-in with optional single sign-on; a mobile web app (QR-MOBILE-WEB-APP); a public API with tokens; company registry lookup; built-in branded templates; e-mail through the company's own mail server with a delivery log; and scheduled backups.

## Rationale

A public help center of an established product in this domain was read in full (436 articles) and recovered as draft artifacts, with provenance pointing to private research notes that are not redistributed. All behaviour is described in Raccord's own words.

The product owner then reduced the scope to what a single small company running its own installation needs. Capabilities outside that scope were removed from the definition; any of them can return later through a new Product Change.

The first implemented slice (project billing to an immutable, numbered invoice) is part of the definition. The project status follows a commercial lifecycle on projects, with physical progress tracked on packing lists.
