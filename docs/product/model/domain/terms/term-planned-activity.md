## Definition

A scheduled occurrence of work for a crew member (a function assignment or a shift) against which hours can be registered and compared. Activities show planned time and cost alongside registered time and actual cost.

## Distinguish From

Registration activity: the registered side.

## Usage

Registration from schedule, reconciliation, freelancer planned cost.
