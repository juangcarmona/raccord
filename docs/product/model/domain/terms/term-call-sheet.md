## Definition

A generated project document containing the project's time schedule and the planning of every crew member and vehicle, intended for team leaders. Settings: template, letterhead, time zone, custom text, file name, email subject, expansion of combinations and VAT display.

## Distinguish From

Personal crew schedule document: one person.

## Usage

Briefing team leaders on site.
