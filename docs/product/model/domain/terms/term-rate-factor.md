## Definition

One band of a crew rate, covering worked hours up to a threshold: a fixed amount for reaching the band and/or an hourly amount for each hour within it. Hourly amounts may be expressed as a percentage of a previous band's hourly amount. Bands build day rates, half-day rates, minimum charges, multi-day fixed rates (24, 48, 72 hours) and overtime steps.

## Distinguish From

Equipment price factor: a duration multiplier for equipment.

## Usage

Calculating planned and actual cost and price from hours.
