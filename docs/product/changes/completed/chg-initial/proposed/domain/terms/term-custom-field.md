## Definition

A workspace-defined additional attribute added to a kind of record: projects, equipment and planned equipment, contacts and contact persons, crew members and scheduled crew, vehicles and scheduled transport, default functions, time registrations, additional costs, shifts and tasks. Attributes: name, owning record kind, entry type (for example yes/no, text, number, date, drop-down, hyperlink, linked item pointing to a crew member or contact), default value, group, searchable flag and confidential flag. Values can be filtered, shown as columns, printed on documents, imported and exported. Shown but not editable in the mobile web app.

## Distinguish From

Tag: untyped label. Built-in attribute: fixed by the product.

## Usage

Capturing company-specific facts such as 'lunch provided' or 'project manager'.
