## Constraint

Raccord is an internal, all-in-one toolkit that one company clones, installs and configures for itself on its own infrastructure; it is open source under AGPL-3.0-or-later, documented for installation on a single server and on Azure, and never depends on a vendor-hosted service as the source of truth. It is not offered as a multi-company hosted service.

## Rationale

Self-imposed product direction and license.

## Consequences

One company per installation; no marketplace, cross-company network or SaaS licensing features; installation, backup and restore are product use cases.
