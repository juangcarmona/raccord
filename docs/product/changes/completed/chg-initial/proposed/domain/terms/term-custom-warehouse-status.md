## Definition

A warehouse status defined by the workspace to mirror its own handling steps (in transit, cleaning, check-up). Custom statuses are ordered among the built-in ones, count as out of the warehouse for availability, have no financial effect, and can be deleted only when nothing references them.

## Distinguish From

Not a project status.

## Usage

Booking targets on the warehouse board.
