## Definition

A reusable additional cost template (name, prices, quantity, discount, VAT class, ledger, details), organised in folders and searchable, from which project additional costs are created.

## Distinguish From

Additional cost on a project: a copy that can be changed without affecting the default.

## Usage

Speeds up adding recurring costs such as accommodation or per diems.
