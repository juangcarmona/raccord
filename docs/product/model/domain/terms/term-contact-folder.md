## Definition

A node in the workspace's Contacts-specific folder hierarchy. A contact may be unfiled or assigned to one contact folder.

## Distinguish From

Contact tag: a grouping label that can span folders. A contact folder does not contain another record type.

## Usage

Organizing the contact directory into root folders and subfolders.
