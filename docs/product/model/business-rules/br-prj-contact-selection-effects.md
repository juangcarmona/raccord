## Rule

Selecting a contact as client fills in its address and default contact person; selecting it as client or location shows its warning and adds its automatic project note; selecting a location shows distance and travel time.

## Rationale

Stored knowledge about a party is applied without effort.

## Examples

See SB-PRJ-CONTACT-WARNING-AND-NOTE.

## Exceptions

None.
