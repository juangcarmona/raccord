## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The public homepage explains the current product clearly

The Spanish homepage SHALL describe the implemented customer-to-invoice workflow in direct language, label its GitHub link accessibly, and keep sign-in and maturity disclosures accurate.

#### Scenario: A production team evaluates Raccord

- **WHEN** a visitor reads the Spanish homepage
- **THEN** they can identify the supported production audience, current invoice workflow, required sign-in, lack of a hosted demo and public registration, and limits on legal or fiscal claims
