## Definition

A user-defined, space-free label that can be assigned to one or more contacts for grouping and filtering the Contacts directory.

## Distinguish From

Contact folder: the optional location of a contact in the Contacts folder structure. A contact tag does not apply to another record type.

## Usage

Classifying contacts by business need, such as region or priority, and filtering the directory by matching tags.
