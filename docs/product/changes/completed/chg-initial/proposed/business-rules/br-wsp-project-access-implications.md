## Rule

Access to the projects module always lets users view projects they are account manager of; granting the crew planner automatically grants access to projects; the crew planner shows all projects visible to the user in projects.

## Rationale

Planning and account management are impossible without seeing the projects concerned.

## Examples

- Granting crew planner access to a role makes the projects module available to it.

## Exceptions

None.
