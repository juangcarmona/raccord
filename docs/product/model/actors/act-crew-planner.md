## Purpose

Staffs jobs with people and vehicles.

## Goals

- Every function on a job is filled by an available, qualified person.
- Crew know where and when to work.

## Responsibilities

- Defines functions on jobs, invites and assigns crew and vehicles, resolves conflicts, approves hours and leave.

## Boundaries

Does not define equipment or issue invoices.
