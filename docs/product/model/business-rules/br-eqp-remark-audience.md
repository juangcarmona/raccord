## Rule

An item's external remark appears on customer documents (quotes, contracts, invoices) and its internal remark on packing slips, never the other way round.

## Rationale

Customer-facing text and crew instructions differ.

## Examples

- Handling instructions in the internal remark show on the packing slip but not on the quote.

## Exceptions

None.
