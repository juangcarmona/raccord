## Definition

The qualified electronic certificate of the issuing company (or of its legal representative) installed in Raccord and used to authenticate submissions of billing records to the tax authority and exchanges of B2B e-invoices.

## Distinguish From

User sign-in credentials; the invoice fingerprint.

## Usage

Configured by an administrator; its expiry is monitored.
