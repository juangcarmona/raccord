## Rule

When the company forces password protection for online quotes, every online quote page requires its password before its content is shown, and the configured background image is displayed on the password page.

## Rationale

Commercial offers must not be readable by anyone holding the link.

## Examples

See SB-DOC-ONLINE-QUOTE-PASSWORD-REQUIRED.

## Exceptions

None.
