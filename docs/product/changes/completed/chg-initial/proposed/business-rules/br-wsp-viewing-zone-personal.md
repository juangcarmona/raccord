## Rule

Changing one's viewing time zone changes neither other users' views nor the workspace default, and requires multiple time zone support.

## Rationale

Personal perspective without side effects.

## Examples

- A planner switches to Tokyo time; colleagues keep seeing Amsterdam time.

## Exceptions

None.
