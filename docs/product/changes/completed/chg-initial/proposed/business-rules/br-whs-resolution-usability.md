## Rule

When solving a shortage the product presents each resolution kind marked usable or not usable for that shortage; an alternative can still be searched and added even when no alternative is configured.

## Rationale

Guides the planner to options that can actually work.

## Examples

No alternative configured: the alternatives option is marked not usable but search remains possible.

## Exceptions

None.
