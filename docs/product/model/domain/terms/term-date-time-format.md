## Definition

How dates and times are written: the time format (24-hour or 12-hour with AM/PM) follows the country chosen at set-up; the date order (day-month-year or month-day-year) follows the regional settings of the user's computer.

## Distinguish From

Not the time zone.

## Usage

Display of all dates and times.
