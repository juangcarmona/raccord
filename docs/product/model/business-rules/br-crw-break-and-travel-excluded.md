## Rule

Break and travel time in a registration are deducted from its duration and are not counted in worked hours; travel time is shown separately so the company can decide whether to compensate it.

## Rationale

Worked time must reflect work.

## Examples

See SB-CRW-WORKED-HOURS-NET-OF-BREAK-AND-TRAVEL.

## Exceptions

None.
