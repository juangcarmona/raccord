## Rule

Registering from planned activities lists the activities the person was scheduled on in the past 24 hours and fills from and until from the chosen ones.

## Rationale

Quick registration right after work.

## Examples

After a night show, the show and load-out functions are offered.

## Exceptions

The list in the mobile web app follows the days-back limit instead.
