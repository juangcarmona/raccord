## Definition

A multiplier applied to an equipment item's unit price according to the length of its usage period in days, so that longer rentals cost less per day (for example 2.8 day-prices for five days). Looked up in the factor group of the item and editable on the project line.

## Distinguish From

Discount: the factor scales the price by duration before discounts; it is not a percentage reduction.

## Usage

Applied automatically when equipment is planned; synonymously called a multiple-day discount.
