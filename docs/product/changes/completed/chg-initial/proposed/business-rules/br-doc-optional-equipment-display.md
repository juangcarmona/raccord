## Rule

Optional equipment is listed among the other equipment by default; a quotation may instead list optional items in a section of their own.

## Rationale

Customers should see clearly what is offered as an option.

## Examples

See SB-DOC-OPTIONAL-EQUIPMENT-SEPARATE-SECTION.

## Exceptions

None.
