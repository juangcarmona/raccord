## Definition

A VAT treatment for, typically, foreign business customers where the invoice charges 0% and the customer accounts for the tax.

## Distinguish From

Zero-rate class (goods exempt from VAT).

## Usage

Selected through a reverse-charge VAT scheme on the customer, project or invoice.
