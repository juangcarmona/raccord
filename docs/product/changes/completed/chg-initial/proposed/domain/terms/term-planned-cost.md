## Definition

The cost of a project as currently planned before it starts: rates of the crew members actually scheduled, rates of the vehicles assigned, purchase prices of sale equipment and the costs of additional costs and expenses.

## Distinguish From

Estimated cost (based on functions only) and actual cost (based on registered hours).

## Usage

Lets the planner check the budget before the job begins and still adjust the plan.
