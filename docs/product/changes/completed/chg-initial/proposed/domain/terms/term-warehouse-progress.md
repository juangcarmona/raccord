## Definition

An indicator of a project's warehouse situation: grey when nothing is in the warehouse yet, green when on track, red when at least one packing list is behind schedule. On the board, a packing list shows red when equipment was left behind, yellow when some equipment is in a different status than the list, green when all equipment is in sync with the list's status.

## Distinguish From

Not the equipment planning (shortage) indicator.

## Usage

Project overview and board.
