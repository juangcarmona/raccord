## Purpose

A person holding the Admin role, who configures the company's Raccord installation.

## Goals

- Users have the right access.
- Settings, number series, taxes, templates and integrations reflect how the company works.

## Responsibilities

- Manages users and the four roles (Admin, Office, Warehouse, Crew) and their permissions, company details, financial settings, document branding, mail account, calendar feeds, API tokens and backups schedule.

## Boundaries

Does not run day-to-day jobs; operating the servers is the installation administrator's concern (often the same person in a small company).
