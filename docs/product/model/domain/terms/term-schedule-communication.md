## Definition

Deliberate sending of planning to crew members for a chosen period and projects: each recipient receives only their own functions with times, locations and remarks, through their preferred channels, optionally with a custom message. The product marks crew members whose planning changed since it was last communicated.

## Distinguish From

Making planning final: visibility only, no message.

## Usage

Informing crew after planning or after changes.
