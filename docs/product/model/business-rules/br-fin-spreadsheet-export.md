## Rule

A data export produces one spreadsheet with the selected records and exactly the columns chosen, including custom columns and extra fields; optional groups (contact persons, crew rates, activities) are marked with a suffix in the column name.

## Rationale

Users take data to other tools.

## Examples

Exporting customers with the phone of the default contact person.

## Exceptions

None.
