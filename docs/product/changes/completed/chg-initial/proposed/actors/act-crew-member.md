## Purpose

Own staff or freelancer who works on jobs.

## Goals

- Know assignments early.
- Declare availability and register hours with little effort.

## Responsibilities

- Responds to invitations, maintains availability and profile, registers worked hours, requests leave, uses the mobile app.

## Boundaries

Sees only what their permissions allow; does not plan others.
