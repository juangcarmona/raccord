## Definition

The classification of priced project content used for subtotals, category discounts, ledgers and profit: rental equipment, sale equipment, crew, transport, additional costs and crew expenses. The revenue of a category is the sum of the totals of its planned content.

## Distinguish From

Ledger account: an accounting destination that by default follows the category but can be changed per item; cost group: a user-defined grouping of additional costs only.

## Usage

Rows of the financial overview, basis for category discounts and for the default ledger assignment.
