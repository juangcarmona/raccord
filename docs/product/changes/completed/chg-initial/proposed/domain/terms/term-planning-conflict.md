## Definition

A situation where a crew member is planned on work they cannot do: double booking on overlapping functions or shifts, or assignment during a period in which they are unavailable. Conflicts typically appear after a project's times shift.

## Distinguish From

Unknown availability: not a conflict.

## Usage

Detecting and correcting impossible staffing.
