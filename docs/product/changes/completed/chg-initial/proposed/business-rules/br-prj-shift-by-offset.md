## Rule

Shifting selected periods by an amount moves each start and end by that number of minutes, hours, days, months or years; a negative amount moves them earlier.

## Rationale

Delays and advances are usually uniform.

## Examples

See SB-PRJ-SHIFT-NEGATIVE-OFFSET.

## Exceptions

None.
