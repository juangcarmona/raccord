## Rule

For monthly revenue figures, a project's revenue is divided equally over the days of its planning period and each month receives the share of its days.

## Rationale

Projects spanning months must be split fairly.

## Examples

See SB-PRJ-REVENUE-SPLIT-ACROSS-MONTHS.

## Exceptions

None.
