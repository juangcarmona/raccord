## Rule

Quotes and contracts follow the same generation, versioning, numbering, validity, sending and signing rules; only their name and number series differ.

## Rationale

Companies choose their own commercial wording.

## Examples

A contract can be regenerated as a new version exactly like a quote.

## Exceptions

None.
