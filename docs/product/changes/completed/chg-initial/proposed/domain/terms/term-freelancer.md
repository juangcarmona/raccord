## Definition

A crew member who works for the company through their own business rather than employment. Their record carries company details (company name, chamber of commerce number, VAT identification number) and they typically invoice the company for activities they performed; the planned cost of an activity is the amount agreed with them.

## Distinguish From

Own staff: employed crew members whose hours feed payroll. Agency crew record: one record standing for many hired people.

## Usage

Invitations, job board, invoice references on registered activities.
