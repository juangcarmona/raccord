## Rule

A user can view, edit or remove their own hours, other crew members' hours, register hours before working them, see activities and clock in from the mobile web app only as far as their role allows.

## Rationale

Hours drive pay and cost.

## Examples

A crew member without pre-registration rights cannot register tomorrow's hours.

## Exceptions

None.
