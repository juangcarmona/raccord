## Intended Outcome

Every article carries a printed label with its code.

## Entry Conditions

Articles have scan codes; a single-label printer and label stock are available.

## Journey Narrative

The administrator creates a label-sized letterhead and sets up the equipment label, and warehouse staff generate and print labels for selected articles, saving them to the articles.

## Variants and Branches

Labels are exported as PDF to the printer's own software.

## Completion Conditions

Labels are attached to the equipment.
