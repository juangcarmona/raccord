## Rule

Changes to planning, function times or projects do not notify crew members by themselves; crew learn of them only when the planner communicates the schedule or when they consult their own schedule.

## Rationale

Planners control when crew are disturbed.

## Examples

Moving a function after sharing sends nothing until the schedule is shared again.

## Exceptions

None.
