## Definition

An audiovisual job or rental engagement for one customer. Its time schedule has a planning period (from when equipment leaves until it is back, which drives availability) and a usage period inside it (the days that are charged, which drives price); further named phases may be added later. It has a project status, a project type, billable work, planned equipment and crew, and documents.

## Distinguish From

Not an invoice: one project may be billed by several invoices.

## Usage

Owns billable lines; its last day is the operation date of invoices that bill it.
