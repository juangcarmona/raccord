## Definition

A separately editable part of a personal profile:
- Personal details: name, contact details, birth date, administrative data (company name, bank account, chamber of commerce number, external references, VAT number), address and cities where the person counts as local, emergency contact, experience and future activities, languages, nickname, dietary wishes, shirt size and formal attire.
- Qualifications: education, accreditations and certificates, recorded per country.
- Skills: chosen from a list or added as free text.
- Travel: identity card and passport, preferred airports, stations and car rental, bonus cards.
- Documents.

## Distinguish From

Not the crew-member record kept by each workspace.

## Usage

Each section is opened and saved on its own; all are optional.
