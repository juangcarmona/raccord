## Definition

An identifier, stored on a record such as an invoice, that an external system assigned to its counterpart of that record. It lets integrations recognise records they already created or exported.

## Distinguish From

Not the record's own number (invoice number, contact number) and not the public-interface item reference.

## Usage

Readable and writable through the public interface on invoices.
