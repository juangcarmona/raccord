## Rule

The insurance charge equals the insurance percentage times the total price of planned rental equipment before discounts.

## Rationale

The insured value does not shrink with commercial discounts.

## Examples

See SB-FIN-INSURANCE-ON-GROSS.

## Exceptions

None.
