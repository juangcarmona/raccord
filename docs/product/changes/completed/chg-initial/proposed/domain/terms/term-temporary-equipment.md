## Definition

An article added to a single project's plan without existing in the catalog, typically a one-off article. It has no owned stock, so its whole planned quantity is shown as a shortage. It can later be saved into the catalog under a chosen folder.

## Distinguish From

Not a catalog article; not a sale item.

## Usage

Avoids cluttering the catalog with one-off articles.
