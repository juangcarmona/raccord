## Rule

In location-based tax systems, equipment marked not taxable is exempt and equipment marked taxable is charged the applicable location tax rate.

## Rationale

Some goods are exempt from sales tax.

## Examples

Exempt items show no tax on the invoice.

## Exceptions

None.
