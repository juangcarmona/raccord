## Rule

Shifting to a new start date-time moves all selected periods by the difference between the earliest selected start and the new start, keeping their durations and relative positions.

## Rationale

A postponed job keeps its internal structure.

## Examples

See SB-PRJ-SHIFT-TO-NEW-START.

## Exceptions

None.
