## Definition

A manually added amount on an invoice, positive (an extra charge) or negative (subtracted from the total), with description and VAT treatment.

## Distinguish From

Billable line recorded on the project (TERM-BILLABLE-LINE).

## Usage

Used for surcharges, corrections and freestanding refunds.
