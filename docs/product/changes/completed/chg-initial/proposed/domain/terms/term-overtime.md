## Definition

Hours registered on a function beyond the hours planned for it, priced through the higher bands of the crew rate attached to the assignment. Overtime exists only when hours are actually registered.

## Distinguish From

Compensation hours: time off earned for extra hours, not money.

## Usage

Actual cost and reconciliation.
