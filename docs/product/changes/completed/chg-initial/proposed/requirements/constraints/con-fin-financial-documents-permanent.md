## Constraint

Finalized invoices and credit notes cannot be deleted from the product; Spanish law requires that finalized invoices cannot be modified or deleted.

## Rationale

Bookkeeping and invoicing law.

## Consequences

Errors are handled by credit notes and cancellation statuses.
