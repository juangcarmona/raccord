## Definition

The places where a price reduction can be set, from narrowest to widest: on an individual line (equipment, function or additional cost), through the customer's discount-group percentage for an equipment item, on a revenue category of the project (percentage), and on the project total (percentage or fixed amount, or by typing the desired total). Customer defaults pre-fill the category and total levels and discount-group percentages.

## Distinguish From

Price factor, which changes the base price by duration; price lock, which freezes prices rather than reducing them.

## Usage

Explains how the discounts shown in the financial overview and on documents were obtained.
