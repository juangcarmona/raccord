## Definition

A named set of equipment items (for example rigging or audio) for which each customer can be given its own discount percentage. An equipment item belongs to at most one discount group.

## Distinguish From

Revenue category: a fixed classification; discount groups are defined freely by the company.

## Usage

When a customer becomes the client of a project, items in a discount group receive that customer's percentage as line discount.
