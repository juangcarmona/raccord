## Definition

An equipment item in the archived state: out of stock, not plannable, still referenced by historical projects and documents, and listed in the archive of the catalog from where it can be restored or permanently deleted.

## Distinguish From

- Hidden item (planner visibility off): still in stock.

## Usage

Kept for history; restorable.
