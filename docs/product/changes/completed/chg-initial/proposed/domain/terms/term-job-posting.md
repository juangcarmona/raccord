## Definition

A function published to crew members so they can sign up for it themselves instead of being invited. Lifecycle: posted (visible to crew; notifications batched) -> removed (withdrawn by the planner). Each crew member can dismiss a posting from their own view. A sign-up plans the applicant on the function.

## Distinguish From

Crew invitation: addressed to chosen people; a posting is open to all crew who can see it.

## Usage

Filling roles where the exact person does not matter.
