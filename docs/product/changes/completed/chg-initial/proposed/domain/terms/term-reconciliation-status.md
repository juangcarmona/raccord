## Definition

Derived comparison of an activity's registered time with its planned time: planned (in the future), exceeds time (registered longer), aligned (exactly equal), under time (registered shorter), no registration (in the past and nothing registered). Accompanied by planned, registered and difference time, and planned, actual and difference cost.

## Distinguish From

Approval check: graded signals for approvers.

## Usage

Payroll accuracy, budget control, billing transparency.
