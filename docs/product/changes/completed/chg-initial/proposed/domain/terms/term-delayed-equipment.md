## Definition

Equipment of a packing list that has not been checked in by the end of its planning period. The packing list is then shown as delayed automatically. A planner can mark whole equipment groups as delayed with an expected return date and time and a remark, which extends the planning period of the units still out, keeping them unavailable until then.

## Distinguish From

Not a project shift (which moves the whole project's times, usage included).

## Usage

Drives the delayed column on the board and availability extension.
