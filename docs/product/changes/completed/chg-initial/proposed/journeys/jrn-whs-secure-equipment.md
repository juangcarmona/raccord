## Intended Outcome

Every article the job needs is covered by reserved own stock and handed to the warehouse.

## Entry Conditions

A project exists with its schedule.

## Journey Narrative

The coordinator plans articles into groups, gives lines needed only part of the time their own periods, reviews shortages and their kinds, replaces what is missing with alternatives, reserves (or confirms) to secure priority, and marks the project ready to pack.

## Variants and Branches

Competing projects are resolved through reallocation; schedule shifts re-open shortages.

## Completion Conditions

No shortages remain and packing lists appear in the warehouse.
