## Definition

A function of kind transport: the need for one or more vehicles on a project. Its price and cost are expressed per kilometre, fixed, or combined, based on the distance from the warehouse to the project location.

## Distinguish From

Vehicle: the resource. Crew function: priced by time.

## Usage

Transport planning and pricing.
