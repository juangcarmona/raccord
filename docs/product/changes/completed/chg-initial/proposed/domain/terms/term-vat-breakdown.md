## Definition

For each VAT rate on an invoice: the taxable base (sum of line net amounts at that rate) and the VAT amount, with invoice totals derived from them.

## Distinguish From

Line amounts: the breakdown is calculated per rate, not per line.

## Usage

Printed on drafts, issued invoices and invoice documents.
