## Definition

A project expense outside equipment and crew (hotel, flights, parking, meals, damage) with name, purchase price per unit, sale price, quantity, discount, VAT class, ledger account and details. It belongs to exactly one cost group, appears on quotes and invoices in its display order. Lost or defective equipment reported at return is added as an additional cost.

## Distinguish From

Crew expense (attached to a function or crew member).

## Usage

Added from scratch or from default additional costs.
