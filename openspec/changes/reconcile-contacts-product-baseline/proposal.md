## Why

Approved Contacts changes exist only on a historical epic branch, blocking current native refinement. Generic imports and mandatory folder wording also conflict with the accepted bounded Contacts profiles.

## What changes

Apply CHG-CONTACTS-RECOVERY through ProductShape after a digest-bound impact review. Preserve historical decision records and the source revision, refresh reviewed consumer citations, and record the remaining implementation recovery separately.

## Capabilities

### New capabilities

- `contacts-product-baseline`: an accepted, coherent baseline for the approved Contacts delivery slices.

## Impact

Product model, Product Change decisions, current OpenSpec citations and delivery operations records. Application code, runtime migrations and feature acceptance remain separate delivery work.
