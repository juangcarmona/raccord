## Rule

A cancelled project that still has planned crew or pending tasks shows a persistent warning.

## Rationale

People may otherwise turn up for a cancelled job.

## Examples

See SB-PRJ-CANCEL-WITHOUT-ACTIONS-WARNS.

## Exceptions

None.
