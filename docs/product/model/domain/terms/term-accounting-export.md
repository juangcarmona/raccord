## Definition

A CSV or spreadsheet file of issued invoices (with ledger rows, VAT by rate, payment terms, invoice moment, number and customer data) and their payments, produced on demand for the accountant.

## Distinguish From

E-invoice export (structured invoice files).

## Usage

Started by the billing administrator.
