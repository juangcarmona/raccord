## Rule

A template copies the client and location (if any), time schedule, notes and tasks, equipment groups with planned equipment, crew and transport functions, additional costs and financial details (discounts, VAT, refundable deposit), but never the crew members and vehicles assigned to functions.

## Rationale

People's availability differs on every date.

## Examples

See SB-PRJ-TEMPLATE-SKIPS-ASSIGNMENTS.

## Exceptions

None.
