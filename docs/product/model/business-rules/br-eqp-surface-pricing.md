## Rule

For a surface item, the price of a planned line is derived from the width times height entered when planning, proportionally to the catalog's standard size and its price.

## Rationale

Area-based goods are sold or rented by size.

## Examples

- Standard 1 m by 1 m at 10: planning 2 m by 3 m prices 60.

## Exceptions

None.
