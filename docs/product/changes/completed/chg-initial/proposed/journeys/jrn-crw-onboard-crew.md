## Intended Outcome

Crew are in the product, can log in, have rates and documents.

## Entry Conditions

The installation is being set up or new people join.

## Journey Narrative

The planner imports crew with login off, enables login when ready so invitations go out, crew set up profiles, the planner links rates and stores contracts and certificates.

## Variants and Branches

Single crew members are added one by one instead of imported.

## Completion Conditions

Crew can be planned and costed.
