## Definition

The company's general conditions, held as one PDF file, attached to e-mails of quotes, contracts and invoices according to a per-document-type setting and shown to customers who sign digitally.

## Distinguish From

Additional conditions: plain text printed in the document itself.

## Usage

Configured once in the financial settings.
