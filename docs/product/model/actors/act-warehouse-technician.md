## Purpose

Prepares, checks out, checks in and maintains physical equipment.

## Goals

- Pack exactly what each job needs.
- Keep stock, locations and condition truthful.

## Responsibilities

- Works packing lists, books equipment out and back manually or by scanning QR/barcode labels with a phone or webcam, reports losses, takes part in stock counts.

## Boundaries

Does not change prices, invoices or project commercial status.
