## Definition

The quantity by which a planned line's demand exceeds what is available to it during its planning period. A shortage belongs to a planned line and therefore to a project; it carries a shortage kind that explains its cause, and can be resolved with a shortage resolution. A project with any shortage shows its equipment planning progress as failing.

## Distinguish From

Not overbooking in the abstract: a shortage is always attached to a planned line. Not a sales shortage, which is a projected stock-out of sale articles.

## Usage

Shown on the project plan, on the warehouse board, on the timeline, and listed across projects for a period.
