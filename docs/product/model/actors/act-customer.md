## Purpose

A person at a customer organization who requests, approves and pays for work.

## Goals

- Receive clear quotes and invoices.
- Approve, sign and pay with little friction.

## Responsibilities

- Submits requests, views and signs quotes and contracts, receives invoices and reminders, pays.

## Boundaries

Has no access to internal planning or other customers.
