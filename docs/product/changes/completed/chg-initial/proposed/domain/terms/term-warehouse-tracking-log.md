## Definition

The chronological record of every warehouse action on equipment: what action, which article, which project, which crew member and when. Filterable by action, project, article and crew member in combination, and by date.

## Distinguish From

Not the audit trail of billing.

## Usage

Tracing misplaced or lost equipment.
