## Definition

A configurable sequence that numbers a kind of record or document (projects, contacts, quotations, contracts, invoices and others) so items can be told apart; each series shows its current number, which is the number of the latest item created in it, and can be adjusted by administrators.

## Distinguish From

Not the invoice series of the billing slice, which is a specialised legally constrained series; not record identifiers, which are internal and immutable.

## Usage

Configured during setup.
