## Definition

Equipment added to a packing list during booking that was not planned, either chosen from the catalog or detected by scanning an unplanned code. It is placed in a new equipment group with configurable price inclusion and times.

## Distinguish From

Not an alternative (which replaces a planned line).

## Usage

Captures last-minute extras so nothing leaves unrecorded.
