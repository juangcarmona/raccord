## Rule

A custom list defined by folder or by tags includes every crew member or vehicle currently in that folder or carrying those tags, including ones added later; individually picked lists change only when edited.

## Rationale

Lists stay current without maintenance.

## Examples

A new freelancer placed in the audio folder appears in the Audio list.

## Exceptions

None.
