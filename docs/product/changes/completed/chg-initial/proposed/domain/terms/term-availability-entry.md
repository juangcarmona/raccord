## Definition

A declared availability of one or more crew members over a period: available, unavailable or unknown, with an optional recurrence and remark. Entered by the crew member for themselves or by someone with the right to do it for others.

## Distinguish From

Appointment: a named commitment; an availability entry carries no activity.

## Usage

Input to the availability state.
