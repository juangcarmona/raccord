## Rule

Functions without connected times do not appear in a project's crew and transport scheduling lists; they can be planned only in the cross-project planner, where undated functions wait in a separate list until placed on the timeline.

## Rationale

Scheduling lists are chronological.

## Examples

A function quoted as 2 days with no dates appears only among unscheduled functions.

## Exceptions

None.
