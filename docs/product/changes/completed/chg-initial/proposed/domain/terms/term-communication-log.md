## Definition

The company-wide list of all communication records, sent by any user and on any channel, filterable by recipient, communication type and channel, and by time period. A narrower sent e-mails view lists e-mail messages only, with their content and delivery status. Visibility of entries depends on the viewer's permissions. The log cannot currently be filtered by project.

## Distinguish From

Project notes; the log covers outgoing communication only.

## Usage

Used to answer whether and when something was sent.
