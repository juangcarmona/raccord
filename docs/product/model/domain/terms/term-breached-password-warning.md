## Definition

A warning shown at registration and at sign-in when the chosen password appears in public lists of passwords leaked from other services. The check reveals neither the password nor anything from which it can be derived to the checking service (only a short fragment of its hash is compared). It says nothing about whether the product account itself was compromised.

## Distinguish From

Not an account breach notice; not a password strength rule.

## Usage

Encourages users to change reused passwords.
