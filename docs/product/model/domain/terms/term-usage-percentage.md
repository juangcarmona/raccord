## Definition

For an equipment item in a selected period: total planned hours (quantity planned times planned duration within the period) divided by the plannable capacity (quantity owned times the period length), expressed as a percentage.

## Distinguish From

Availability (whether units are free at a moment).

## Usage

Column of equipment statistics.
