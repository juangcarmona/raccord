## Definition

The commercial settings stored on a customer contact and proposed whenever it becomes the client of a project or an invoice: discount percentages for crew, transport, rental equipment, sale equipment and the total; percentages per discount group; default payment term; default invoice moment; VAT scheme or tax rate.

## Distinguish From

The values on a project, which are copies taken when the client was selected and are not updated afterwards.

## Usage

Maintained per customer; applied with the user's confirmation on client selection.
