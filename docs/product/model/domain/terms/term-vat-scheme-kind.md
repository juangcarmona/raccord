## Definition

The way a VAT scheme assigns rates: per class (each class has its percentage), fixed rate (one percentage for everything, overriding classes) or reverse charge (0%, tax accounted by the customer).

## Distinguish From

VAT class.

## Usage

Chosen when defining a scheme.
