## Rule

Marking lost equipment as found increases stock again but does not change any costs already charged to the customer; credits must be made separately.

## Rationale

Financial documents are not changed by warehouse actions.

## Examples

- A receiver invoiced as lost and later returned needs a credit note issued by hand.

## Exceptions

None.
