## Definition

A record that units did not return: article, quantity, date, optional charge to the customer. Lifecycle: Lost (stock reduced) to Found (dated; units counted back into availability).

## Distinguish From

Not the charge: the charge is a separate amount billed to the customer.

## Usage

Loss overview in the warehouse.
