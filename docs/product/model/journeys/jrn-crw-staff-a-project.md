## Intended Outcome

Every function of a project is filled by suitable people and vehicles who know when and where to work.

## Entry Conditions

A project with a time schedule exists.

## Journey Narrative

The planner defines the functions, checks availability, invites crew, receives answers that plan people automatically or in draft, completes planning directly (including vehicles), finalises it and shares personal schedules.

## Variants and Branches

- Job board instead of invitations.
- Retiming the project creates conflicts that are resolved before sharing.
- Reservations secure people before details are known.

## Completion Conditions

All functions are fully planned and visible; crew have received their schedules.
