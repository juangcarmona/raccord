## Purpose

Runs audiovisual jobs for customers and records the work that will be billed.

## Goals

- Every job has its customer, dates and billable work recorded as the job evolves.
- Nothing worked is forgotten at billing time.

## Responsibilities

- Creates projects for customers.
- Records billable work (equipment, labour, transport, consumables, services) with quantities, prices, discounts and VAT rates.

## Boundaries

Does not issue invoices or define the issuer's legal details.
