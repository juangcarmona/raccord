## Definition

A chronological record of changes to a project (also kept for equipment, contacts and crew member data), showing for each save who saved and when, and per change whether something was added, modified or deleted, organised by area, group, item and field with old and new values. Saves are grouped in one-hour intervals per user. Changes are only recorded from the moment the log is activated for the workspace. The crew member log excludes schedule changes.

## Distinguish From

Audit event: the compliance trail of invoicing actions.

## Usage

Accountability and troubleshooting of planning changes.
