## Definition

A link or button in a generated document leading the recipient to a web page where the document can be signed. Company settings govern it: whether customers may sign at all, a logo shown on the signing page, whether the signer may add a comment, and whether expired documents may still be signed. Signed copies are stored with the project.

## Distinguish From

Signed quote (TERM-SIGNED-QUOTE): the commercial result of signing a quotation or contract; the element is the means of collecting the signature and works on other document types too.

## Usage

Present on quotations, contracts and slips that may be signed.
