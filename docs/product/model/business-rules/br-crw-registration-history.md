## Rule

Each change to a time registration is recorded with when it happened, who made it and the resulting status, and the owner can be notified of changes made by others.

## Rationale

Transparency for crew whose hours are corrected.

## Examples

A supervisor shortens a night shift; the technician sees who changed what.

## Exceptions

None.
