## Definition

A configurable tile on a personal dashboard that summarises an area and links to its detail. Kinds: personal schedule (today and tomorrow), tasks (public, personal or both, including overdue), open invoices (open, overdue, to be invoiced), revenue per month per status as bar chart (relative range up to 15 months back and forward, status selection) and as pie chart (with month-over-month change), invitations (function, date, time, duration, expiry, remarks; accept or decline), and time registration (past functions without registered hours). A user can own several named dashboards; the default dashboard contains invitations, time registration, personal schedule and tasks.

## Distinguish From

Statistics: full analytical reporting.

## Usage

Personal start page.
