# Research notes

Research notes contain dated findings. They are inputs to product discovery, not copied product documentation and not legal advice. Keep source links and dates for official and legal sources.

Capability research on commercial products is used only to understand the problem space. Product-specific provenance (URLs, notes) stays in the git-ignored `.private/research/` folder of the maintainer who did the research and is never published; public notes describe the domain in Raccord's own words.
