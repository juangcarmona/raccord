## Definition

A delivery or reduction of stock entered with a future date. The quantity counts toward availability only from that date, so expected purchases can be planned against and planned disposals are no longer offered after their date.

## Distinguish From

- Stock correction: immediate reset to a new level.

## Usage

Used for announced deliveries and planned sales or disposals.
