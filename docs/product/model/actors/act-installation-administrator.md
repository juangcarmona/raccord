## Purpose

Installs and operates a Raccord installation for one company.

## Goals

- Install quickly from documented steps.
- Back up and restore without losing or altering issued records.

## Responsibilities

- Configures the installation, keeps it healthy and upgraded.
- Takes and rehearses backups and restores.

## Boundaries

Does not create business records; does not alter issued invoices or audit history.
