## Rule

Overviews present projects distinctly by status group: unconfirmed (inquiry, draft, pending), confirmed (confirmed, in progress, execution done, complete) and cancelled (shown struck through in calendar and timeline).

## Rationale

Status must be readable at a glance.

## Examples

In the calendar a cancelled project is struck through and an unconfirmed one de-emphasised.

## Exceptions

None.
