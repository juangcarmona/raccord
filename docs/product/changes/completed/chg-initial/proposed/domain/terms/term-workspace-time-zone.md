## Definition

The time zone of the workspace, set automatically from the company's location at set-up and changeable by administrators; it is the reference for planning, and new projects start in it. Changing it affects the entire workspace.

## Distinguish From

Not the viewing time zone a user selects, and not a project's own time zone.

## Usage

Planning baseline for all users unless multiple time zones are enabled.
