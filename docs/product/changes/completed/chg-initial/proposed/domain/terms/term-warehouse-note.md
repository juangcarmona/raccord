## Definition

A named note attached to a project, packing list or booked article for the crew, with a visibility (everyone or account managers only) and chosen display places (details, warehouse, schedule).

## Distinguish From

Not a project remark printed on quotations.

## Usage

Crew instructions such as keep returning chairs at the dock.
