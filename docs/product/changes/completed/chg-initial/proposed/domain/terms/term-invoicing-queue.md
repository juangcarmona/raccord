## Definition

The list of confirmed projects with an invoice moment part whose trigger date falls in a selected period and that is not yet invoiced, highlighting parts whose date has passed. A second list shows what was marked as invoiced externally. Each entry shows the share to invoice.

## Distinguish From

Invoice list (existing invoices); billing state of a billable line.

## Usage

Daily work list of the billing administrator.
