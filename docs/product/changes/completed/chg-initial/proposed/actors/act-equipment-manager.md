## Purpose

Owns the equipment catalog and stock master data of the company: what exists, how it is structured, priced and tracked, and how much of it there is.

## Goals

- The catalog reflects every item, kit and serial unit the company owns or sells.
- Stock quantities, locations and values are accurate enough to plan and to report on.

## Responsibilities

Maintains the equipment catalog: items, kits and their default contents, accessories, alternatives, prices, codes and labels, stock quantities and stock counts.

## Boundaries

Does not plan equipment on projects or book it in and out of the warehouse; issues no invoices. The role may be held by the same person as the warehouse technician in small companies.
