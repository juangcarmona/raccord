## Definition

The four roles every installation starts with; each can be edited and further roles can be added:
- Admin: may view and change everything, including configuration and roles.
- Office: sees all modules and may change what is needed to keep track of projects, planning, finances, contacts and equipment.
- Warehouse: works with equipment, stock, packing and returns, and sees the projects those tasks concern.
- Crew: given automatically to new users; sees their own schedule and the relevant details of the projects they are planned on.

## Distinguish From

Not custom roles, which administrators add themselves.

## Usage

Starting point for access configuration.
