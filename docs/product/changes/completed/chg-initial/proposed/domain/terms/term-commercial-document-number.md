## Definition

The identifier of a quote or contract, taken from a configurable number series whose starting number the company chooses, used for internal reference and by the recipient.

## Distinguish From

Invoice number (TERM-INVOICE-NUMBER), which follows legal gapless numbering.

## Usage

A new number means a new, separate offer.
