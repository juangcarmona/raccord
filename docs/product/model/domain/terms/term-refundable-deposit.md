## Definition

A fixed amount requested from the customer as security for rented goods, with a deposit status that tracks whether it has been paid and returned. It is not shown on documents unless the template includes it, and it is refunded through a credit note.

## Distinguish From

Insurance charge (a non-refundable percentage); down payment through a partial invoice.

## Usage

Recorded on the project financial overview.
