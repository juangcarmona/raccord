## Definition

The number of units of an article a planned line needs. It is the demand used in the availability forecast for the line's planning period, and the quantity to be packed on the corresponding packing lists. For physical combinations the planned quantity counts combinations; content follows.

## Distinguish From

Not the reserved quantity (the part of the planned quantity that has secured stock) and not the booked quantity (what has physically moved in the warehouse).

## Usage

Set when planning; compared with availability to detect shortages; shown while booking together with the quantity still to book.
