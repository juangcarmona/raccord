## Rule

A crew member record flagged as usable several times at the same time can be planned on overlapping functions without being treated as double booked.

## Rationale

Hired people from an agency are planned under the agency's name.

## Examples

The agency record is planned on three overlapping stagehand functions; no conflict is reported.

## Exceptions

None.
