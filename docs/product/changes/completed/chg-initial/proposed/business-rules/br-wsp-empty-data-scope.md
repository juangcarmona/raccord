## Rule

Emptying the workspace deletes projects, equipment and equipment folders, contacts, vehicles, time registrations, tasks, notes, invitations, appointments, reservations, saved statistics, all generated documents, default functions, project templates, crew availability, sent emails, email and text templates, tags, notifications and custom fields; it keeps crew members, document templates, letterheads, settings, presets and user roles.

## Rationale

Start clean without losing set-up work on people and configuration.

## Examples

- After emptying, the crew list and roles are intact but there are no projects.

## Exceptions

None.
