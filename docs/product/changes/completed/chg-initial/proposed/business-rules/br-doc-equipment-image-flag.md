## Rule

An equipment article's image appears on documents that list equipment only when that image is marked to be displayed in documents.

## Rationale

Articles can hold internal photos that are not meant for customers.

## Examples

See SB-DOC-EQUIPMENT-IMAGE-FLAGGED.

## Exceptions

None.
