## Definition

The single legal entity that issues invoices from a Raccord installation, identified by legal name, tax identifier and complete postal address.

## Distinguish From

Not a user of the product, and not a customer.

## Usage

Printed as the invoice issuer; its details are snapshotted at issue time.
