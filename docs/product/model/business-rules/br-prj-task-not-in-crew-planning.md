## Rule

Tasks are shown on boards, the task area, a project's tasks and the tasks widget, never in crew planning, which shows only time-bound commitments (functions and shifts).

## Rationale

Crew planning must reflect who is committed at a given time.

## Examples

A twelve-hour design task does not block the designer's availability.

## Exceptions

None.
