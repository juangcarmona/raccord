## Definition

A named tariff for crew work. Attributes: kind (price or cost), scope (global, linkable to many crew members, or individual to one crew member), rate factors (hour bands with fixed and/or hourly amounts) and status. A simple form also exists with an hourly rate, a daily rate and a maximum number of daily hours. A crew member can have several linked rates (one per skill) and one default.

## Distinguish From

Crew expense: ancillary costs such as meals and travel. Equipment price factor: multiplies equipment day prices.

## Usage

Function prices, planned and actual crew costs, overtime.
