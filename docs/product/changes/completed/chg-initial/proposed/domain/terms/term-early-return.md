## Definition

A check-in that happens before the end of the line's planning period. The returned quantity is released from the project's reservation (the planned quantity is unchanged) and becomes reservable by other projects.

## Distinguish From

Not a plan change: the plan still shows the original quantity.

## Usage

Frees stock sooner.
