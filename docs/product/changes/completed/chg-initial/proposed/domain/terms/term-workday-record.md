## Definition

The legal record of one employee's working day: the clock-in and clock-out moments and the breaks between them, with the device and place the event was registered from, and every correction made afterwards. Entries are never overwritten: a correction is a new entry that references the one it corrects, with the reason and who made it. Lifecycle per day: Not started → Working → On break ↔ Working → Finished; a day still Working at its end becomes Open (unclosed) until corrected.

## Distinguish From

Time registration (project hours): what was worked on which project, edited and approved for costing and payroll. The workday record is the legal attendance record and can prefill project hours.

## Usage

Clocked by every employee, office staff included; reviewed by managers; exported for the inspectorate.
