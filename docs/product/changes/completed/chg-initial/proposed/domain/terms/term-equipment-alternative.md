## Definition

A catalog article declared usable in place of another, for example two similar loudspeakers. Alternatives are kept per article in the catalog and can be added while solving a shortage, in which case they are saved for reuse. Replacing a planned line by an alternative chooses how many units are replaced and whether the original line price is kept or the alternative's price applies.

## Distinguish From

Not an accessory and not combination content.

## Usage

Used to solve shortages and to swap articles during packing.
