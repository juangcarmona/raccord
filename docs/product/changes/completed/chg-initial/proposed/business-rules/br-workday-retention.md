## Rule

Workday records and their corrections are kept for at least four years and remain exportable even after the employee leaves the company.

## Rationale

CON-WORKDAY-RECORD-REGULATION.

## Examples

Records of an employee who left in 2026 are available until at least 2030.

## Exceptions

None.
