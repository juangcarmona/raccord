## Definition

For an article, the quantity returning from one project that can go straight out to another without being shelved: the overlap between the amount returning and the amount leaving within a chosen window of 12, 24 or 48 hours, when the first project ends before the second starts and both are confirmed or later.

## Distinguish From

Not a reservation transfer: it is a handling optimisation.

## Usage

Shown per article with returning, leaving and overlap quantities.
