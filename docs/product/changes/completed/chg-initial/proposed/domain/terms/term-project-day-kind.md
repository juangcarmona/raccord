## Definition

A classification of the days of a project used to filter calendars and day views: equipment out (equipment leaves the warehouse), equipment in (equipment comes back), need crew (days with crew functions), need transport (days with transport functions), and every project day.

## Distinguish From

Time period: user-defined interval.

## Usage

Personal schedule and per-day filters.
