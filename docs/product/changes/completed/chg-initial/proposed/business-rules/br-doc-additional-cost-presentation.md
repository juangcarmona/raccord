## Rule

The additional-costs section of a document hides cost groups by default; when groups are switched on it shows each group's name and subtotal; it always ends with the total of all additional costs including discounts.

## Rationale

Keeps existing layouts unchanged while allowing more detail.

## Examples

See SB-DOC-ADDITIONAL-COST-GROUPS-SHOWN.

## Exceptions

None.
