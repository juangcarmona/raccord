## Rule

A broad role such as Office does not by itself grant access to all data: each permission of any role can still be limited.

## Rationale

Account managers may need to change only their own invoices.

## Examples

- An office-type role may edit own invoices but only view colleagues' invoices.

## Exceptions

None.
