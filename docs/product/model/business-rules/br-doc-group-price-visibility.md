## Rule

An equipment group of a project marked as not shown in the price calculation is omitted from quotations and invoices but still listed on packing slips.

## Rationale

Customers need not see consumables such as cables that the warehouse must still pack.

## Examples

- A cables group set to not shown appears on the packing slip only.

## Exceptions

None.
