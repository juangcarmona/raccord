## Rule

Usage percentage = (planned hours / plannable item-days) / 24 x 100, where planned hours are quantity planned times planned duration within the period and plannable item-days are the quantity in stock times the period length in days.

## Rationale

Measures how much of the fleet earns money.

## Examples

See SB-FIN-USAGE-PERCENTAGE.

## Exceptions

None.
